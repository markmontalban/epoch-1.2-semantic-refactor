# [[runs/run-013/RUN-013-Sales-Playbooks|RUN-013]] Report — Tiered Sales Playbooks

Last updated: 2026-07-20 18:22:25 PDT — edited by: Codex

[[runs/run-013/RUN-013-Sales-Playbooks|RUN-013]] now holds a source-backed, internal sales/research/marketing system
for ten distinct contexts. It identifies what must be true before a route earns
further qualification; it does not establish demand, buyer intent, product
efficacy, pricing, a pilot, or permission to contact or publish.

## Result

- **Tier 1 is clear:** CCBHC and integrated FQHC cards are the two deepest routes because their operating models allow a credible organization-level qualification question. [CCBHC](01-CCBHC-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md) · [FQHC](02-FQHC-INTEGRATED-BH-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md)
- **Tier 2 stays conditional:** Non-CCBHC community behavioral health, HCBS/DSP, peer/recovery, MHA/comparable workforce, and caregiver organizations have a complete internal playbook but need setting, sponsor, or governance proof before active sales development.
- **Tier 3 stays parked:** Nursing homes, home health/hospice, and independent LCSWs are retained as risk-aware routes; each needs an explicit re-opening condition before account qualification.

## What every card now contains

- **Decision snapshot:** Tier, confidence, boundaries, and advance/park condition prevent a source-rich card from becoming an unfounded market claim.
- **Sales and pilot logic:** Each card names a flexible staff-wellbeing position, role hypotheses, discovery questions, objections, safe staff-only cohort, and pass/fail/stop conditions—without price or outcome promises.
- **Marketing hypotheses:** Each card includes source-aware positioning, three content angles, LinkedIn/association, owned-channel, and segment-specific route ideas, all clearly draft-only and unauthorized for publication.
- **Evidence and agent interface:** Every card contains 3–5 annotated sources, source limitations, fact/hypothesis/unknown labels, a disqualifier, and a copyable public-research brief. [Contract](PLAYBOOK-CARD-GUIDE.md) · [Matrix](SOURCES.md)

## Tier map

| Tier | Contexts | Appropriate next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CCBHC; FQHC integrated behavioral health | Approve, revise, or defer a fixed-sample public organization/role qualification pass. |
| 2 | Non-CCBHC behavioral health; HCBS/DSP; peer/recovery; MHA/comparable workforce; caregiver organizations | Choose one setting/program boundary only after Tier 1 learning creates a reason to branch. |
| 3 | Nursing homes/SNF; home health/hospice; independent LCSWs | Keep parked; re-open only when a named safety/product-fit evidence gap is resolved. |

## Guardrails and next move

- **Sources are context:** The [source/claim matrix](SOURCES.md) distinguishes primary, sector, internal, and market-language sources and records what none of them prove.
- **Web content is untrusted:** Agent briefs require fact extraction only, ignore embedded instructions, and prohibit authentication, contact collection, monitoring, scraping, outreach, CRM work, pricing, and publishing.
- **Next smallest decision:** Select a fixed public sample for the CCBHC/FQHC comparison and define its organization-level fields, disconfirmers, and advance/park threshold in a separate approved run; do not add named-person research or external action.
