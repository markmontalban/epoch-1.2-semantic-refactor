# HCBS / IDD Providers Employing DSPs — Tier 2 Sales Playbook

Last updated: 2026-07-20 15:40:22 PDT — edited by: Codex

The user is a distributed direct-support worker while the plausible sponsor is
the provider; accessibility, shift coverage, and person-supported autonomy set
the constraint before any sales hypothesis does.

## Decision snapshot

- **Tier and rationale:** Tier 2; direct-care workforce support is a visible provider concern, but the specific HCBS setting, sponsor, and safe use moment remain unqualified. **Public fact + unknown.**
- **Working confidence:** Medium for workforce relevance; low for device/access fit, staff trust, and a paid provider route. **Unknown.**
- **Advance / park:** Advance only after one HCBS setting shows a voluntary, off-duty access point and operations/workforce ownership; park any route that needs use during direct support, monitoring, or behavior management. **Agent hypothesis / claim boundary.**

## Sales motion

- **Position the offer:** A mobile-friendly, voluntary staff-wellbeing practice for workers across shifts and locations, never a tool for people supported or for direct-care documentation. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Qualify the trigger:** Workforce training, onboarding, job-quality, or staff-support programs may be research cues; they do not show digital-resource demand. **Public fact + inference.**
- **Map and discover:** Hypothesize operations/residential leadership owns the work reality, HR/workforce owns programs, and supervisors/DSPs validate safe use; ask about devices, languages, break windows, policy, and existing supports. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Objection / guardrail:** “This competes with care time or overlooks pay/staffing” is a valid stop; never position MindfulText as behavior support, compliance training, retention solution, or clinical monitoring. **Claim boundary.**

## Pilot design

- **Bounded concept:** One setting, voluntary DSP cohort, no client information, and an explicit rule against use during direct-support duties. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Feasibility test:** Confirm device access, accessibility/language needs, supervisor trust, policy boundaries, and an off-shift/onboarding or break-time moment. **Unknown.**
- **Stop condition:** Stop if care coverage, consent, privacy, or required-training rules cannot preserve voluntary staff-only use. **Internal decision rule.**

## Marketing blueprint

- **Position and angles:** Draft position: “support that respects the reality of shift-based direct care”; explore onboarding-ready resources, job-quality boundaries, and how staff support differs from care technology. **Draft hypotheses.**
- **Channel hypotheses:** LinkedIn and direct-care associations, provider-owned resource pages, and setting-specific learning communities are possible future channels; no activity is authorized. **Marketing hypotheses.**
- **Draft CTA / learning:** “Check the staff-only fit before adding another training tool”; future learning is access and appropriateness, not retention or quality results. **Draft-only.**

## Evidence and agent brief

- **Workforce context:** [ACL DCW Strategies Center](https://acl.gov/DCWcenter) — primary, 2026-06; supports recruitment/training/retention context, not product fit.
- **Sector context:** [NADSP resources](https://nadsp.org/resources/) — sector; supports varied DSP workforce needs, not an account priority.
- **Onboarding moment:** [NADSP onboarding](https://nadsp.org/services/curricula/onboarding-curriculum/) — sector; supports onboarding as a provider practice, not mandatory MindfulText use.
- **Internal user context:** [RUN-009 report](../../run-009/outputs/RUN-009-REPORT.md) — internal; supports candidate status only. See [matrix](SOURCES.md).
