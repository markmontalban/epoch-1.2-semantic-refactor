# MHA Affiliates / Comparable Support Workforce — Tier 2 Sales Playbook

Last updated: 2026-07-20 15:40:22 PDT — edited by: Codex

Affiliates vary in program model and authority. This is a mission-fit research
route, not a national MHA sales motion or a new-target account pool.

## Decision snapshot

- **Tier and rationale:** Tier 2; affiliate education, services, and peer-support work create potential program containers, but local variation prevents generalization. **Public fact + inference.**
- **Working confidence:** Medium for mission adjacency; low for program fit, sponsor, funding, and authority to offer a resource. **Unknown.**
- **Advance / park:** Advance only where a specific affiliate publicly shows a compatible education/workforce program and local governance; park any route that competes with mission, peer support, or clinical care. **Claim boundary.**

## Sales motion

- **Position the offer:** An optional, mission-aligned staff or facilitator resource that complements—not substitutes for—education, peer support, or clinical/community programs. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Qualify the trigger:** Professional education, staff learning, or workforce-support programs are research cues; national affiliation is not an account-level trigger. **Public fact + inference.**
- **Map and discover:** Hypothesize local program/workforce owner and mission/clinical reviewer, with executive/funder feasibility review; ask which program could house an opt-in resource and what would harm trust. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Objection / guardrail:** “This dilutes our mission or duplicates peer support” is valid disconfirmation; never imply national endorsement, treatment benefit, or a uniform affiliate buyer. **Claim boundary.**

## Pilot design

- **Bounded concept:** One local program, voluntary staff/facilitator cohort, clear consent model, and local mission-appropriateness review. **Agent hypothesis.**
- **Feasibility test:** Confirm actual program scope, resource channel, accessibility, brand concerns, and a local owner before a design discussion. **Unknown.**
- **Stop condition:** Stop if any mission, peer-support, or clinical boundary is contested. **Internal decision rule.**

## Marketing blueprint

- **Position and angles:** Draft position: “a small practice that supports the people supporting the community”; explore mission-fit review, education-resource design, and local variation. **Draft hypotheses.**
- **Channel hypotheses:** Affiliate/professional-education communities, LinkedIn thought leadership, and a local-program owned-resource page are possible only after separate approval. **Marketing hypotheses.**
- **Draft CTA / learning:** “Check whether this fits one existing local program”; future learning is mission fit and local ownership, not network-scale demand. **Draft-only.**

## Evidence and agent brief

- **Affiliate variation:** [MHA affiliates](https://mhanational.org/our-affiliates/) — sector, current; supports local advocacy/education/service variation, not a national sales path.
- **Network context:** [MHA affiliation overview](https://www.mhanational.org/sites/default/files/Intro%20to%20MHA%20National.pdf) — sector, historical; supports varied functions, not a budget.
- **Peer-learning context:** [SAMHSA technical assistance](https://www.samhsa.gov/technical-assistance?page=1) — primary; supports community learning structures, not product fit.
- **Internal boundary:** [SEG-008](../../../growth/SEGMENTS.md#seg-008--community-mental-health-support-workforce) — internal; preserves non-targeting limits. See [matrix](SOURCES.md).
