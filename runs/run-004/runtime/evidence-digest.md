onboarding, refresher training, and staff growth; it describes Clinical Academy
development after new-employee orientation. Official business-opportunities

implementation RFP. This documents public workforce-training activity only;

## R4-E04 — HRSA behavioral-workforce strain

Opened HRSA/NCHWA 2023 and 2025 briefs: low wages, high workloads/caseloads,
workplace violence, and limited organizational support are retention pressures.
The 2025 brief also reports persistent shortages and maldistribution, especially
in rural areas, and says burnout and reimbursement barriers hinder workforce
accessibility. This is national category and structural context only—not proof
of any specific organization's pain, authority, readiness, or MindfulText
demand. Origin: `GO20-05`.
