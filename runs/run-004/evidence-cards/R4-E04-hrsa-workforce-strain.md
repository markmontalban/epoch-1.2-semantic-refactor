# R4-E04 — HRSA behavioral-workforce strain

Last updated: 2026-07-14 01:57:32 PDT — edited by: Codex

**Sources:** HRSA/NCHWA [2023 brief](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/sites/default/files/bureau-health-workforce/Behavioral-Health-Workforce-Brief-2023.pdf) and [2025 brief](https://bhw.hrsa.gov/sites/default/files/bureau-health-workforce/data-research/Behavioral-Health-Workforce-Brief-2025.pdf), both opened 2026-07-14.

**Observed facts:** HRSA identifies low wages, high workloads/caseloads, workplace violence, and limited organizational support as retention pressures. Its 2025 brief reports persistent shortages and maldistribution, especially in rural areas, and says burnout and reimbursement barriers hinder workforce accessibility.

**CDP facet:** category and structural context. **Limit:** National workforce evidence does not establish a specific organization's pain, buying authority, readiness, or demand for MindfulText.
