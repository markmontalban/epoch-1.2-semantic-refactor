# RUN-005 — Care-delivery organization registry report

Last updated: 2026-07-15 00:50:44 PDT — edited by: Codex

RUN-005 closed within its four-hour ceiling with public, organization-level
research only; it creates a reusable registry and targeting model, not a CRM,
buyer list, demand claim, or authorization to contact anyone.

## Result

- **Fifty-six durable records:** The registry holds `ACC-001`–`ACC-056`, with stable IDs, state, segment/subsegment, care model, normalized source references, research tier, confidence, last-verified date, and explicit unknowns. [Registry](../../../growth/account-registry/organizations.csv)
- **Source trail preserved:** Sixteen public sources sit in a separate ledger, so later research can add rather than overwrite evidence. [Source ledger](../../../growth/account-registry/sources.csv)
- **No personal dossiers:** The output contains organization-level functions and titles only—no names, emails, phones, individual profiles, or inferred authority. [Registry guide](../../../growth/account-registry/README.md)
- **Run boundary held:** No outreach, monitoring, scraping, login, paid provider, CRM write, publishing, or local-model work occurred.

## What the registry says

- **Six qualify-first accounts:** Centerstone, Santé Group, Gulf Coast Center, Heritage Behavioral Health Center, SERV Behavioral Health System, and NYC Health + Hospitals Behavioral Health have public workforce or operating context worth a later, separate refresh. It is not buyer intent.
- **Thirty-six CCBHC cluster seeds:** Kansas, Oregon, and Oklahoma records were listed as state-certified/demonstration CCBHCs in the January 2024 directory, making them structured comparison candidates rather than validated accounts.
- **Fourteen directory leads:** Illinois records and Southwest Guidance Center are deliberately lower-confidence because their directory basis is grant/status context that needs current first-party verification.
- **FQHC remains comparative:** HRSA and NACHC sources support keeping `SEG-003` as a distinct comparison branch; RUN-005 does not conclude that CCBHCs are the best buyer segment. [Segment context](../../../growth/SEGMENTS.md)

## Buyer titles to validate

- **Executive and economic sponsors:** CEO, Executive Director, President, COO, CFO, and Chief Strategy/Transformation titles may own strategic or funding decisions.
- **Clinical and program sponsors:** Chief Clinical Officer, Chief Medical Officer, VP Clinical Services, Clinical Director, and Behavioral Health Director may assess program/workflow fit.
- **Workforce and implementation sponsors:** CHRO, VP People/HR, workforce-development, learning-and-development, quality, operations, and employee-wellbeing titles may surface an internal champion or implementation owner.
- **Review functions:** Procurement, contracts, grants/finance, CIO/IT, privacy, security, compliance, and legal titles may shape review without necessarily sponsoring a purchase.
- **Role is not authority:** Public title structure only identifies a function to validate later; it does not indicate a person, budget, need, or willingness to pay. [Buyer taxonomy](../../../growth/account-registry/BUYER-TITLE-TAXONOMY.md)

## Decision implications

- **Advance segment research, not outreach:** `SEG-002` now has a broad, structured account universe and a smaller source-backed qualify-first set, sufficient for a future public source-opening pass.
- **Refresh before ranking harder:** The smallest next research action would open current first-party sources for the six qualify-first accounts plus a balanced sample of directory leads, then confirm organization type, workforce context, and role visibility.
- **Keep claims narrow:** MindfulText may only be investigated as an optional nonclinical, low-disruption support complement; the registry does not support claims about staffing, workload, retention, clinical outcomes, or care quality.
- **No buyer validation yet:** No record meets a level-2 buyer-intent signal, and no manual discovery conversation, marketing action, or partnership is authorized.

## Evaluation

- **Pass on registry construction:** The run met the 50–75 organization target, retained source references, preserved title/function taxonomy, and made uncertainty visible.
- **Inconclusive on market choice:** The evidence does not yet select CCBHC versus integrated FQHC contexts, establish a purchase path, or show willingness to pay.
- **Fail condition avoided:** The run did not rely on private data, unnamed contact enrichment, generic scraping, or unsupported buyer claims.
