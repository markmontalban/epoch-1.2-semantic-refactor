# Executive-Insight Asset Quality Scorecard

Last updated: 2026-07-21 13:42:00 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Rubric

Scores assess the internal research asset, not account fit, buyer intent, attention, or revenue likelihood. Each dimension is one to five.

| Dimension | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Specificity | Does the finding depend on this organization rather than the category? |
| Novelty | Would a generic ICP brief be unlikely to surface it? |
| Executive relevance | Does it illuminate a visible strategic, operating, or governance question? |
| Claim safety | Can it be stated without inferring authority, intent, budget, or outcome? |
| MindfulText boundary fit | Does it identify a plausible non-clinical, staff-only complement or a clear disqualifier? |

## Decision

**Production-feasibility result: pass.** The workflow produced three organization-specific memos, each with three source-checked observations and a meaningfully different recommendation. It passed because it uncovered both a promising premise (Gulf Coast Center) and an important disqualifier (Santé), not because it generated a large volume of material.

**Attention result: untested.** No executive received, opened, replied to, or used any artifact. The proposition that big effort earns attention remains a hypothesis for a separately approved manual interaction.

**Smallest next test:** Build one tightly scoped Gulf Coast Center insight asset, compare it with a conventional account-personalized note, and have Mark approve the exact recipient, channel, asset, message, and stop condition before any contact occurs.
