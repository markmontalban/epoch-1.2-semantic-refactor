# Personalization-Angle Taxonomy

Last updated: 2026-07-21 13:42:00 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Purpose

An executive insight asset should earn attention through a verified, useful observation—not through the amount of research behind it. This taxonomy turns the broad account-research idea into bounded, reusable angles while preserving the difference between public context and evidence of buyer intent.

## Ranked angles

| Angle | Public evidence | Executive question it can illuminate | Safe asset form | Main failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operating change | Merger, expansion, rebrand, service launch | What must remain stable while the organization changes? | Change-context memo | Treating change as purchase intent |
| Strategy and imperatives | Strategic plan, annual report, board update | Which priorities are explicitly owned now? | Priority-to-constraint map | Repeating the strategy page |
| Workforce operating system | Onboarding, leadership development, staffing model, job postings | Where does staff experience affect execution? | Workforce-friction brief | Claiming a program fixes staffing |
| Care-model design | Service continuum, crisis response, CCBHC implementation | Which workflows are protected from added burden? | Boundary-and-fit memo | Crossing into clinical-workflow claims |
| Funding and constraint | Public grant, budget, board, regulator sources | What is expanding, constrained, or time-bound? | Timing/constraint note | Inferring a budget or buying window |
| Governance and accountability | Board agenda, public plan, audit, public meetings | What does leadership have to make visible and govern? | Decision-friction map | Conflating governance with authority |
| Service-capacity expansion | New county, facility, service line, hours, access route | What coordination or training burden accompanies scale? | Expansion-readiness note | Treating a public announcement as demand |
| Existing support programs | Existing wellbeing, training, EAP, crisis-stress, vendor material | Where would MindfulText be redundant, complementary, or inappropriate? | Complement-vs-redundancy assessment | Ignoring a clear disqualifier |
| Category and regulatory context | Government, funder, or primary-sector source | Which external condition makes the internal issue timely? | Context overlay | Using category evidence as account evidence |
| Public language and framing | Repeated organization wording across sources | Which terms signal the organization’s stated model? | Language map | Mimicking language without substance |
| Public ecosystem relationships | Institutional partners, counties, associations, public programs | Which organizational routes shape implementation? | Ecosystem map | Collecting or scoring named people |
| Public event/activity surface | Public conference or meeting agenda | What shared topic may make an insight timely? | Timely discussion premise | Monitoring or inferring personal interest |

## Retention rule

Retain an observation only when it is organization-level, source-linked, current enough for the decision, and explains a specific leadership or operating question. Two sources are preferred for a consequential inference. Named-person profiles, contact details, private data, and inferred authority remain out of scope.

## Asset quality rule

The asset should contain three to five observations, one explicit uncertainty, and one clear reason the observation matters. A large dossier without a new decision-relevant conclusion is research theater, not an attention asset.
