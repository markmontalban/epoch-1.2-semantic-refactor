Last updated: 2026-07-28 10:44:41 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Outcome

The Social Studio is now defined as a founder-led storytelling system with
**mindfulness as the root and human systems as the recurring story tension**.
It is designed to preserve Mark's breadth without making every interest a
separate audience promise or channel.

The recommended operating choice is to keep Mark's personal Instagram personal
and treat the existing AI Instagram/TikTok pair as the provisional public
surface. Do not create several new accounts. This is a capacity and identity
recommendation, not a platform-algorithm claim. No source found establishes
that people Mark knows will suppress recommendations; block/remove only for a
real privacy, safety, or psychological-boundary reason.

## What this run established

- A durable worldview statement and story format in
  [[runs/run-018/outputs/SOCIAL-STUDIO-OPERATING-MODEL|the operating model]].
- A channel job architecture that gives the public account, private account,
  and MindfulText-owned channels different purposes.
- A human-agent contract that makes agents a research/story/learning room while
  keeping Mark responsible for voice, ethics, community judgment, and action.
- A bounded six-story bridge as the smallest future test shape—not an approved
  publishing plan.
- A dependency gate: Pulse cannot become a content engine or public proof asset
  until [[runs/run-019/RUN-019-Pulse-Public-Asset-Audit|RUN-019]] has qualified
  its provenance and public-use boundary.

## What remains unproven

- Whether the existing AI account can stretch into the broader story world
  without confusing followers.
- Which lens, format, or cadence generates relevant repeat attention.
- Whether content has any product, acquisition, retention, or “stickiness”
  effect. That thought is not yet atomic enough to promote to a growth
  hypothesis.
- Whether the Pulse corpus can support public aggregate insights, scale claims,
  or a searchable landing page.

## Mark's next decision

Choose one:

1. **Baseline:** approve a separate six-story manual baseline on the existing
   AI account pair.
2. **Tighten:** refine the public promise or first audience before testing.
3. **Park:** retain the Social Studio as direction and do not create content
   work now.
