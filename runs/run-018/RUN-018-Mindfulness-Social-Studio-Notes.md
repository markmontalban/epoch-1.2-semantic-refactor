## Working notes

- **Founder direction retained:** Mindfulness is the underlying theme; AI,
  startup/accelerator exposure, healthcare, and film are sources of scenes and
  tension rather than separate brands.
- **Account context retained:** Mark reports an existing AI Instagram and TikTok
  presence of roughly 170 followers each. The count, handle, audience makeup,
  and performance history were not inspected or verified in this run.
- **Evidence distinction:** Current official platform explanations describe
  recommendation systems using predicted viewer behavior and content signals;
  they do not establish that known followers suppress reach. The case for
  retaining a personal account is therefore a privacy/psychological-boundary
  choice, not an algorithm tactic. See [[runs/run-018/outputs/SOURCES|SOURCES]].
- **Architecture insight:** A memorable public identity needs one repeatable
  tension, not a new account for every interest. The proposed tension is what
  fast, ambitious systems make people miss—and the small choice that restores
  attention, agency, or care.
- **Agent boundary:** Agents may source permitted references, organize a story
  backlog, draft options, and summarize approved learning. Mark owns worldview,
  lived material, final voice, community judgment, and every public action.
- **Dependency:** [[runs/run-019/RUN-019-Pulse-Public-Asset-Audit|RUN-019]]
  must qualify any Pulse-derived content. It is not a default content feed.
