## Decision

This run should help decide whether Direct Support Professionals represent a sufficiently distinct and researchable MindfulText audience to advance as a focused ICP hypothesis, while preserving the distinction between frontline users and organizational buyers.

## Tasks

- `R5a` Synthesize prior-run evidence relevant to care-delivery workforce stress, retention, low-disruption support, MHA context, and buyer ownership.
- `R5b` Verify the public meaning, role boundaries, and care settings associated with “Direct Support Professional.”
- `R5c` Identify recurring DSP workforce problems, decision-maker functions, and language relevant to a non-clinical stress-support product.
- `R5d` Compare DSPs with the existing LCSW and care-delivery audience records; recommend whether to add an ICP, split a segment, or park the idea.
- `R5e` State the cheapest next evidence step and the claims that remain unproven.

## Evaluation

- **Pass:** The report defines DSPs behaviorally, distinguishes user from buyer, cites multiple public source types, and makes a clear ICP/segment recommendation.
- **Fail:** The output treats DSPs as a generic synonym for all care workers or relies on unsupported wellness/buyer claims.
- **Inconclusive:** Public evidence defines the role but cannot distinguish a useful MindfulText situation or plausible organizational owner.

## Facts, assumptions, hypotheses, unknowns

- **Fact:** Prior runs established workforce capacity, retention/burnout, emotional load, and low-disruption workflow as recurring care-delivery themes; no prior run created a DSP ICP.
- **Founder context:** Mental Health America uses “Direct Support Professionals” in relevant decks/materials; the founder-supplied MHA Dutchess evidence is prioritization context, not transferable proof.
- **Agent hypothesis:** DSPs may be a distinct frontline user audience because their work is continuous, relational, and often non-clinical, while the likely sponsor is an organization-level workforce, operations, or program leader.
- **Unknown:** Whether DSP employers would fund MindfulText, whether DSPs would engage repeatedly, which DSP setting is best, and whether the MHA terminology transfers beyond MHA-affiliated contexts.
