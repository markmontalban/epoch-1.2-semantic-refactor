# Personal Experience Prototype Brief

## Recommendation

Prototype **Your MindfulText Rhythm** as a guided reflection that becomes a
small personal practice page. The reflection gives an immediate moment of
value before a trial or paid choice; the page makes the first experience
concrete and controllable.

Do not imply that an opaque system is developing a personal program. Say
plainly that the member has created an initial Rhythm and can change it at any
time.

## V0 journey

```text
Invitation / landing page
  → 4–6 guided-reflection prompts
  → “Your MindfulText Rhythm” page
  → trial or paid choice
  → first configured practice / first text
  → optional explicit reactions refine the next suggestion
```

Every prompt has a short reflective sentence, one simple choice, and a clear
statement of what that choice shapes. The sequence should take about two
minutes total.

| Step | Prompt shape | Member choice | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “What would you like to make a little more room for this week?” | One current focus | Initial practice theme |
| 2 | “When could a small pause feel most doable?” | Preferred moment | Suggested delivery window |
| 3 | “What kind of support feels realistic today?” | Brief reflection, guided pause, or reminder | Initial format mix |
| 4 | “How would you like your Rhythm to feel?” | Gentle, grounding, curious, or encouraging | Tone preference |

Do not ask about diagnosis, symptoms, medications, trauma, therapy, crisis, or
other sensitive health matters.

## Minimum personal page

- A member-confirmed focus statement.
- One next practice and two optional micro-practices from approved content.
- Editable Rhythm settings: timing, format, and tone.
- An explanation: “We use the choices and reactions you share to suggest what
  comes next.”
- Controls to change the focus, reset the Rhythm, and delete preferences.

Use **Your Rhythm**, **Today’s pause**, **Choose your next step**, and **Shape
what comes next**. Avoid “program,” “treatment plan,” “assessment,”
“diagnosis,” and “algorithmically optimized care.”

## One-focus design

[[product/PRODUCT-IDEAS|PRO-015]] belongs in V0. One focus reduces choice overload, gives the member a
recognizable story about why the experience is theirs, and constrains content
selection to an explainable rule. It is a preference—not a promise—and the
member may change or clear it at any time.

## Staged delivery

| Stage | Scope | What it can answer | What it must not claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Static click-through with synthetic choices | Is the flow understandable? | Conversion or retention |
| 1 | Deterministic reflection-to-page mapping | Can the value exchange be delivered honestly? | That adaptation improves outcomes |
| 2 | Explicit “more like this” / “not now” reactions | Are chosen controls usable? | That hidden inference is needed |
| 3 | Broader personalization or a live experiment | Requires separate approval and privacy review | Clinical or efficacy outcomes |
