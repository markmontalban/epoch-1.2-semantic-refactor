# [[runs/run-016/RUN-016-Reflective-Onboarding|RUN-016]] — Product-led Onboarding Validation Report

## Verdict: pass for an internal prototype decision; not market-validated

The founder concept is coherent as a single product-led acquisition and
activation loop. A short guided reflection is the value exchange; the
member-controlled personal page turns it into a first MindfulText practice.
[[product/PRODUCT-IDEAS|PRO-014]] and [[product/PRODUCT-IDEAS|PRO-015]] should stay linked to [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-005--reflective-onboarding-to-a-personalized-mindfultext-rhythm|GH-005]], rather than being
treated as an isolated funnel or an unscoped personalization system.

The recommended working term is **Your MindfulText Rhythm**. It communicates an
evolving daily experience without the institutional feel of “texting program.”
“Current focus” is preferable to “goal” because it is lighter, time-bounded,
and avoids a performance or treatment implication.

## Established

- A minimum concept, prompt pattern, page contents, and staged delivery
  boundary are documented.
- V0 can be deterministic and based on visible choices/reactions; an open-ended
  AI profile is neither needed nor justified.
- A future comparison can isolate reflective onboarding from a generic trial
  path with completion, trial-start, first-practice, and seven-day measures.
- Privacy and claim constraints are concrete enough for prototype review.

## Still unknown

- Whether real prospective members find the reflection valuable rather than
  extractive.
- Whether it improves trial start, activation, paid conversion, or retention.
- Which focus choices, prompts, content, timing, and tone are desirable.
- Whether the existing app can support V0 without [[product/PRODUCT-IDEAS|PRO-001]]–[[product/PRODUCT-IDEAS|PRO-004]].

## Next decision

Approve, revise, or defer a separate Stage 0 clickable prototype. That decision
must name the design surface, synthetic versus participant input,
reviewer/participant boundary, data retention, and success criteria. A live
website, payment, text-message, analytics, or participant test requires its
own explicit authorization.
