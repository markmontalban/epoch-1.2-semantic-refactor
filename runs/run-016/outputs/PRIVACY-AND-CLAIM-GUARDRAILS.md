# Privacy and Claim Guardrails

## V0 data minimization

| May be needed | Keep out of V0 |
| --- | --- |
| One selected current focus | Diagnosis, symptoms, medication, therapy history, trauma, crisis status, or other sensitive-health content |
| Member-chosen time window, format, and tone | Hidden mood, health, risk, or adherence scores |
| Explicit “more like this,” “less like this,” or “not now” reactions | Inferences from browsing or unrelated page behavior |
| Account/entitlement state when the underlying system is approved | Sharing preference/history data with third parties without a separate basis and disclosure |

## Required controls

1. Explain which choices shape the first experience.
2. Make focus and preferences visible and editable from the personal page.
3. Offer a reset to generic settings.
4. Provide a deletion path consistent with the approved account policy.
5. Never require sensitive disclosure to access a useful initial experience.

## Claim boundary

The experience may invite reflection, offer a nonclinical mindfulness practice,
and describe chosen preferences. It must not promise to diagnose, treat,
prevent, cure, improve clinical symptoms, or deliver efficacy from a chosen
focus or personalized sequence. It must not imply that the personal page is a
clinical profile or care plan.

Use: “You can shape what comes next with the preferences and reactions you
choose to share.”

Avoid language that suggests hidden inference, professional judgment, or proven
individual outcomes, such as “Our AI understands what you need” or “your plan
is clinically tailored.”
