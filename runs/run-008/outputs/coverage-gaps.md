## What is still unknown

- **Article-level frequency.** The recurrence counts in the narrative and pain tables are coded source-profile mentions, not counts of published articles, views, or audience prevalence.
- **Audience overlap.** The map does not establish whether the same leaders, clinicians, workers, or consumers follow multiple sources.
- **Buyer ownership.** A source may reach a care-delivery worker or executive without revealing who owns a workforce, wellbeing, workflow, or technology budget.
- **Small-creator durability.** Cadence, audience size, and current activity are not independently verified for many podcasts, newsletters, and creators.
- **Local coverage.** State and city-level journalists, newsletters, and community voices remain under-sampled relative to national and association sources.
- **Setting-specific language.** The same words—burnout, access, coordination, workforce—can describe different tasks and economics in a hospital, CCBHC, FQHC, hospice, home-health, or SNF setting.
- **Evidence of solution demand.** Public narrative recurrence is not validation of willingness to buy, efficacy, or MindfulText demand.
