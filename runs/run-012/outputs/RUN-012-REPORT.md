# [[runs/run-012/RUN-012-Segment-Prioritization|RUN-012]] Report — Top-ten Segment Prioritization

Last updated: 2026-07-20 10:45:00 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Result

**Pass, with a narrow recommendation:** retain [[growth/SEGMENTS#seg-002--community-behavioral-health-providers|SEG-002]] CCBHC/community
behavioral-health work as the primary account-qualification route and use
[[growth/SEGMENTS#seg-003--fqhcs-with-integrated-behavioral-health|SEG-003]] FQHC integrated behavioral health as the backup comparison. The map
also identifies eight other candidate contexts, but none has enough retained
evidence to displace those two without additional work.

## What this establishes

- Ten distinct candidate contexts can be compared without collapsing the user,
  sponsor, workflow, and claim boundary into a generic “caregiver” market.
- CCBHCs are the strongest immediate research route because the repo already
  contains a 56-organization, public-source registry and role taxonomy; the
  setting also has an externally legible organizational model. [Registry](../../../growth/account-registry/README.md) · [SAMHSA](https://www.samhsa.gov/communities/certified-community-behavioral-health-clinics/ccbhc-certification-criteria)
- FQHC integrated behavioral health remains the best backup because it tests
  whether the thesis survives a distinct operating and governance setting.

## What remains unproven

- No organization has expressed interest, named a budget, confirmed a buyer,
  agreed that MindfulText fits its workflow, or authorized a conversation.
- The ranking does not authorize account selection for outreach, named-person
  collection, AI personalization, message sending, CRM action, or monitoring.

## Next decision

Approve, revise, or defer a **separate public account-and-buyer-role
qualification run** for a small matched CCBHC/FQHC sample. Its output should
be organization facts, role-based buying-process hypotheses, disconfirmers,
and a recommendation on whether a manual market-test packet is warranted.
