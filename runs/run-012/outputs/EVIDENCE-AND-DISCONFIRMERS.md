# [[runs/run-012/RUN-012-Segment-Prioritization|RUN-012]] — Evidence and Disconfirmers

Last updated: 2026-07-20 10:45:00 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Evidence that changed the ranking

- **CCBHC organizational legibility:** SAMHSA's certification criteria require
  staffing planning, care coordination, quality/reporting, governance, and a
  defined service scope. This supports a clearer organization-level research
  frame than the broader behavioral-health category. It does not prove budget
  or fit. [SAMHSA](https://www.samhsa.gov/communities/certified-community-behavioral-health-clinics/ccbhc-certification-criteria)
- **FQHC comparison is viable:** HRSA's UDS materials explicitly cover
  staffing/utilization and behavioral-health integration. This makes FQHCs a
  meaningful comparison setting, not a generic health-care add-on. It does
  not reveal a sponsor or interest in a wellbeing program. [HRSA](https://bphc.hrsa.gov/data-reporting/uds-training-and-technical-assistance/staffing-utilization)
- **DSP branch remains real but immature:** DOL describes DSPs as a distinct
  direct-care workforce and acknowledges a nationwide shortage. [[runs/run-009/RUN-009-DSP-Qualification|RUN-009]]
  already found that a provider employer must remain separate from the worker
  audience. No setting has been selected and no direct MindfulText evidence
  exists. [DOL](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/odep/program-areas/individuals/DSP)
- **Long-term care is a disconfirming contrast:** CMS staffing concern is
  strong, but that makes the risk of confusing a small supportive practice with
  structural staffing and care obligations unusually high. It is a poor first
  qualification route. [CMS](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-safety-standards/quality-safety-oversight-general-information/cms-nursing-home-staffing-campaign)
