## Method

Each candidate is scored 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) on public evidence only.
Scores are comparative research-priority judgments, not forecasts of demand,
revenue, budget, authority, adoption, or willingness to talk.

## How to read the result

- **Primary — CCBHCs:** The existing 56-account registry, role taxonomy, and
  evidence spine make this the most efficient place to test a specific
  organization-sponsored workforce hypothesis. SAMHSA's current criteria make
  staffing, care coordination, quality/reporting, and governance visible
  organizational contexts; this does **not** mean a clinic needs or would buy
  MindfulText. [SAMHSA criteria](https://www.samhsa.gov/communities/certified-community-behavioral-health-clinics/ccbhc-certification-criteria)
- **Backup — integrated-behavioral-health FQHCs:** HRSA's current reporting
  structure makes behavioral-health integration and staffing a visible,
  comparable organizational context. This is the strongest independent
  comparison branch, but it has less retained account evidence and a less
  proven fit than CCBHCs. [HRSA staffing and utilization](https://bphc.hrsa.gov/data-reporting/uds-training-and-technical-assistance/staffing-utilization)
- **Closest alternative — DSP employers:** Direct Support Professionals are a
  distinct workforce with documented shortage pressure, but [[runs/run-009/RUN-009-DSP-Qualification|RUN-009]] left the
  provider setting, sponsor, workflow, and voluntary-use conditions unresolved.
  [DOL DSP overview](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/odep/program-areas/individuals/DSP)

## Guardrail

No score is a buyer-intent signal. The next allowed action is a separately
bounded, public account-and-buyer-role qualification pass—not named-person
collection, personalization, message sending, CRM action, or automation.
