# [[runs/run-017/RUN-017-Social-Search-Route|RUN-017]] — Social/Search Route Qualification

Last updated: 2026-07-21 13:16:27 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Recommendation

Advance one constrained route—not an employee-account program or a broad SEO
bet. Use a clearly MindfulText-owned character account only as a later,
separately approved distribution surface for one people-first founder asset.
The asset should extend the existing CCBHC readiness strategy: explain what a
voluntary staff-support resource can complement and, more importantly, what it
must not be expected to fix.

This creates a coherent bridge: gentle satire earns attention around the
familiar "wellness will fix a structural problem" tension; the owned asset
gives the reader a useful, claim-bounded answer; the existing MindfulText
product position provides the relevant next context. It is a route hypothesis,
not evidence that MindfulText will rank, resonate, convert, or improve an
outcome.

## A. Search route

| Element | Qualified direction | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search topic | Responsible staff support for care teams: the distinction between a voluntary micro-practice and structural workforce/clinical responsibilities. | Do not target generic "funny mindfulness," broad burnout cures, or generic meditation terms. The query check did not show a credible reason to expect a new site to rank for them. |
| Owned asset | One founder article paired with the draft CCBHC readiness checklist from [[runs/run-014/RUN-014-CCBHC-Site-Strategy|RUN-014]]. The article answers a concrete reader question: what a staff-support resource should never be asked to do. | No live page, CTA, form, health claim, case-study statistic, or search-ranking promise is authorized. Exact MHA Dutchess proof remains separately controlled. |
| Search rationale | The asset is specific to MindfulText's existing healthcare-professional and flow-of-work position, while giving a useful answer even to a visitor who never buys. | Google recommends people-first content rather than material made mainly to attract search traffic; SEO is an aid to discovery, not the purpose. |
| What to measure later | Query impressions and click-through for the exact asset; referral visits from the character account; qualified asset engagement; a deliberately defined commercial-learning event. | No metric is collected in this run. Reach alone is diagnostic, not a continuation criterion. |

## B. Brand-owned character architecture

The account should be a brand property, not a simulated independent creator or
a disguised employee account. Its name, bio, and every product-adjacent post
should make the relationship plain—for example, "A MindfulText character" or
"from the MindfulText team" in natural platform-appropriate wording.

An employee may perform the character only under a voluntary, written internal
agreement that covers time/compensation, attribution, boundaries, approvals,
and a stop/exit path. That agreement is outside this run. FTC guidance indicates
that an employment relationship needs disclosure with the endorsement itself;
an account bio alone is not enough. This is an operational constraint and not
legal advice.

### Satire guardrails

- Aim the joke at bad incentives, performative busyness, and wellness-as-a-
  substitute-for-structural-support—not at patients, care workers, people in
  distress, or specific employers.
- Make the brand/narrator part of the joke. Do not moralize, mock vulnerability,
  manipulate fear, or imply that a text message fixes staffing, pay,
  supervision, crisis care, treatment, or burnout.
- Keep the asset's useful explanation separate from the joke. A social post may
  create curiosity; the linked asset must stand on its own.
- Do not present fictional employee/customer experiences, clinical claims,
  performance claims, testimonials, or outcome statistics without separately
  verified authority.

## C. Measurement design for a later baseline

| Layer | Minimum defined signal | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant attention | Saves/shares or constructive comments from an intended reader group, assessed manually against a short rubric. | Tests whether the premise is legible beyond raw views. |
| Search discovery | Exact-query impressions and clicks to the one owned asset, only after an approved page is published and Search Console is available. | Tests whether the asset earns discoverability; it does not prove ranking competitiveness. |
| Commercial learning | One predeclared event such as a relevant organization-path page view or voluntary readiness-checklist request. | Indicates whether attention reaches a product-relevant context; it is not buyer intent. |

Pre-register the audience, platform, post/time ceiling, asset URL, disclosure
wording, voice rubric, and stop condition before publication. Do not pool
unrelated views, comments, or branded searches into a success claim.

## D. Decision

The route passes **concept qualification** because it is consistent with the
existing MindfulText position and [[runs/run-014/RUN-014-CCBHC-Site-Strategy|RUN-014]] asset strategy, preserves the
structural-support boundary, and has a transparent character architecture.
It does **not** pass a market test: no asset is live, no platform is selected,
no account exists, and no audience/search/commercial signal has been observed.

The next smallest test, if Mark separately approves it, is one manual social
baseline: one named platform, one approved existing-or-published asset, a small
post/time ceiling, conspicuous disclosure, and manual review of the three
signal layers above. Do not launch multiple employee accounts or start a broad
content/SEO calendar.
