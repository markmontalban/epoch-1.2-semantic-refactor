# [[runs/run-020/RUN-020-CCBHC-Buyer-Context-Brand|RUN-020]] — CCBHC Stakeholder Day in the Life

## Reading this map

These are role-based operating archetypes, not personas of real people. The CCBHC criteria establish a setting with staffing, access, coordination, quality, and governance pressures; the rhythm below is a synthesis of those obligations and retained CCBHC work. A stated daily pattern is an **agent hypothesis** unless the linked source makes it a public fact.

| Function | Day-in-the-life pressure and attention window | What the person needs to hear | Language likely to fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | **Hypothesis:** moves between mission, service access, staffing, financing, governance, and escalations; can evaluate a new idea only when its organizational role is unmistakably narrow. | “Optional staff support that sits beside structural workforce work; no patient or clinical workflow.” | “Fix burnout,” “transform retention,” or anything that competes with staffing and care obligations. |
| Workforce / learning sponsor | **Hypothesis:** balances recruiting, retention, benefits, training, policy, and the question of whether a program adds work. | “A voluntary, low-lift practice that can fit an existing learning or wellbeing moment.” | Generic self-care, a mandatory program frame, or an unexamined promise of engagement. |
| Clinical / quality reviewer | **Public context:** staffing, scope, care coordination, quality, and reporting are formal CCBHC areas. **Hypothesis:** this reviewer first checks clinical boundary, safety, and implementation burden. | “Not treatment, crisis support, documentation, patient engagement, or a quality-reporting system.” | Clinical-adjacent claims, implied outcomes, or workflow ambiguity. |
| Operations sponsor | **Hypothesis:** sees whether the offer survives shift coverage, handoffs, onboarding, and the actual time available. | “Use only in an optional break, between-shift, onboarding, or chosen learning window.” | “Fits any workflow,” or a claim that ignores coverage and staffing realities. |
| Finance / procurement | **Hypothesis:** tests category, funding, contract burden, and whether a small offer creates a large vendor-review obligation. | “A narrowly defined staff resource with clear boundaries and no implied clinical integration.” | Vague ROI, inflated savings, or hidden implementation assumptions. |
| Privacy / IT / compliance | **Hypothesis:** asks what data exists, where it flows, whether integration is implied, and whether policy review is required. | “No patient data, EHR role, care coordination, or reporting function is proposed.” | “Simple” without a data description, or an assumption that low friction removes review. |

## Aggregate life-context rule

The requested age, income, location, and home-life context belongs in the map only as a statistical frame:

- **Age:** report a weighted median and range by occupation proxy, not one invented CCBHC-buyer age.
- **Income:** distinguish national occupational earnings from household income; neither is an employer-specific wage or a purchasing-power claim.
- **Home life:** report only distributions such as households with children, multigenerational households, and commute/geography context where a reproducible ACS filter supports them. Do not write an imagined spouse, children, commute, housing, or caregiving story for a role.
- **Location:** compare urban, suburban, small-metro, and rural service contexts only where a public CCBHC sample and ACS geography make the comparison explicit.

The current evidence supports this methodological boundary, not a reliable numerical CCBHC-specific average. A later approved statistical pass could calculate weighted proxy estimates using ACS PUMS occupation codes for medical/health services, HR, training/development, financial, computer/information-systems, social/community-service, and executive/operations managers.

## Brand implication

MindfulText earns trust here by showing that it sees the system before offering the small practice: staff wellbeing is not a staffing plan; a voluntary, nonclinical moment may still be a responsible complement when the organization chooses it.
