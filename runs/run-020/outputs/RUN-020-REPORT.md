# [[runs/run-020/RUN-020-CCBHC-Buyer-Context-Brand|RUN-020]] — Report

## Result

**Pass, with a material demographic limitation.** Current public sources
validate the high-constraint CCBHC context assumed by the retained CCBHC work:
staffing, access, care coordination, service scope, quality/reporting, and
governance are formal program areas. They justify a cautious brand translation
that starts with system conditions and makes a small, optional role explicit.
They do not validate demand, budget, buyer authority, clinical impact, or
public resonance.

## What changed

- Added [[growth/BRAND-DIRECTION|BRD-002]] as the provisional CCBHC-specific
  translation of the general `BRD-001` worldview.
- Added a stakeholder day-in-the-life map covering executive, workforce,
  clinical/quality, operations, finance/procurement, and privacy/IT functions.
- Added an evidence boundary for age, income, home life, and location: the
  appropriate output is an aggregate, weighted occupation-proxy analysis, not
  a fictional “average buyer.”

## Decision

Advance `BRD-002` only as an internal voice and positioning guardrail. A
separate approval is needed before any external asset, site change, outreach,
named-account research, demographic analysis, market test, or product claim.
