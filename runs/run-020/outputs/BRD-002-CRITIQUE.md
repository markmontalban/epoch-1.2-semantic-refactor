# [[runs/run-020/RUN-020-CCBHC-Buyer-Context-Brand|RUN-020]] — BRD-002 Critique

## What is stable enough to retain

`BRD-001` supplies a cross-domain founder worldview: mindfulness can be a
constructive force across many domains, while delivery and learning methods
need to evolve. `BRD-002` does not test or replace that worldview. It turns it
into one cautious organizational expression.

## Tensions to keep visible

| Tension | BRD-002 response | What remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad mission vs. narrow route | Makes CCBHCs a lens, not the whole brand. | Whether CCBHC should be the first commercial route. |
| Mindfulness promise vs. clinical inference | Names a private, nonclinical practice and limits the role. | What outcome language, if any, can be substantiated later. |
| Human voice vs. operational credibility | Leads with structural care and concrete boundaries. | Whether actual reviewers find the language useful. |
| Personal context vs. stereotyping | Uses role and aggregate public context only. | Which life-context variables materially improve communication. |

## Red-team findings

- The phrase “staff support” can still be heard as an outcome promise unless
  every asset keeps the complement and nonclinical boundaries nearby.
- “No patient data” describes a desired boundary, not a completed privacy
  review or a universal exemption from vendor review.
- The evidence supports CCBHC operating constraints and some reported
  workforce-wellbeing activity; it does not support a buying path, willingness
  to pay, or resonance claim.
- No actual age, income, household, or location profile should be presented as
  a CCBHC stakeholder fact until an approved weighted ACS role-proxy analysis
  supplies reproducible estimates.

## Conclusion

Keep `BRD-002` as a provisional internal translation. Use it to improve
clarity and eliminate overclaiming, not to authorize external copy, publish an
asset, target a demographic, or claim that the general brand direction is now
settled.
