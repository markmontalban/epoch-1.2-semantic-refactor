# RUN-007 Coverage Gaps

Last updated: 2026-07-15 01:25:00 PDT — edited by: Codex

## Known gaps

- **Local and regional journalism:** The sweep retained national and sector-specific sources but did not systematically enumerate every state, metro, rural, or community-news outlet.
- **Platform-native discovery:** TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Substack, and YouTube search were represented only through publicly indexed landing pages and selected creator sites; no platform monitoring or exhaustive follower ranking was performed.
- **Individual reporter beats:** The registry names only a small number of individual journalists/editors where a public editorial page clearly supported it; it does not contain personal contact details or a complete staff directory.
- **Specialty care:** Pediatrics, maternal care, oncology, emergency medicine, rehabilitation, disability services, and dental care were not separately sampled as their own media ecosystems.
- **Audience validation:** Reach, engagement, buyer-role composition, and openness to contributed content remain hypotheses unless a source publicly documented them.

## Recommended next research slices

1. **SEG-002 behavioral health:** Refresh 8–12 sources and map recurring workforce, CCBHC, retention, and low-disruption-support language.
2. **FQHC/rural care:** Refresh 8–12 sources focused on smaller organizations and community-health-center leadership.
3. **Workforce creator comparison:** Compare 5–8 nursing/clinician creators against 5–8 operator publications using the same audience-fit rubric.
4. **Local source discovery:** Select one state or region and identify local journalists/newsletters covering care-delivery workforce conditions.
