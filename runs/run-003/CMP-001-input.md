## Task

Create a founder-reviewable validation packet for `GH-004 — Low-disruption
workforce reset for community behavioral-health providers`. Do not authorize,
recommend, or imply outreach. Do not claim buyer intent, proven outcomes, or
clinical efficacy.

## Known context

- `SEG-002` contains community behavioral-health providers with public
  workforce-shortage, retention, staff-wellbeing, and operational-capacity
  signals.
- The potential buying group includes workforce/HR, clinical, operations,
  quality, and eventually finance/procurement functions; public research does
  not establish their budget or readiness.
- `EV-001` is internal prioritization evidence that a short daily text program
  can be meaningful in one care-delivery organization. It cannot be quoted or
  generalized without rights confirmation.
- The offer hypothesis is a daily, asynchronous text-based reset that fits a
  one-to-two-minute workday window and complements structural workforce change.

## Required output

1. One-sentence testable hypothesis and its principal disconfirmation.
2. Buyer-group roles and the role each may play.
3. Explicit claim boundaries and proof still needed.
4. Five to seven discovery questions, phrased to learn rather than sell.
5. A proposed level-1 or level-2 target signal, with pass/fail/stop conditions.
6. Draft-only conversation or one-page concept material; no contact names,
   outreach instructions, or ungrounded performance claims.
7. A concise decision recommendation: revise, park, or request Mark approval
   for one manual discovery test.
