## Research question

Which care-delivery contexts have an observable workforce problem and a
plausible buying group for a low-disruption, text-based stress-support benefit?

## Segment map

The broad `SEG-001` market breaks into two initial research branches:

- `SEG-002 — Community behavioral-health providers` is the first active
  branch. National Council and SAMHSA materials make workforce shortages,
  burnout, recruitment, retention, employee wellness, and service pressure
  visible category concerns. The branch contains CCBHC contexts but does not
  assume every CCBHC has the same buyer or budget.
- `SEG-003 — FQHCs with integrated behavioral health` remains a candidate
  branch. Recent FQHC wellbeing research finds stress and resource/time barriers
  but also stresses leadership engagement and structural change; it needs a
  separate buyer/workflow map before a hypothesis is selected.

## Public evidence and contradictions

| Finding | Research implication |
| --- | --- |
| The [National Council’s workforce program](https://www.thenationalcouncil.org/program/center-for-workforce-solutions/) frames burnout, recruitment, retention, and healthier workplace cultures as organizational problems and offers CCBHC workforce resources. | `SEG-002` has a credible, public problem space and language for further account research. |
| The [National Council’s workforce-strategies material](https://www.thenationalcouncil.org/5-workforce-strategies-behavioral-health-organizations/) describes staff shortages, rising acuity, administrative demand, and pressure to do more with less. | The buyer problem is not merely personal mindfulness; the proposition must explicitly complement operational change. |
| A [recent FQHC wellbeing study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/15555240.2026.2635552) found workplace stress and engagement barriers, and points to leadership-engaged, customized, structural approaches. | Do not collapse FQHCs into the first community-behavioral-health hypothesis. |
| [SAMHSA’s organizational-strategies resource](https://library.samhsa.gov/sites/default/files/pep22-06-02-005.pdf) emphasizes organizational contributors to burnout. | No external claim may present MindfulText as a solution for workload, staffing, supervision, compensation, or culture failures. |

## Buying-group map

The initial buying group is an organizational cluster rather than a single
"decision maker":

| Role / function | What it may care about | What public research can establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workforce / HR | retention, benefits, culture, wellbeing | Whether workforce initiatives, hiring, or benefits language is visible. |
| Clinical leadership | care continuity, staff load, ethical/clinical boundaries | Whether the proposition fits care workflow and does not imply clinical treatment. |
| Operations / quality | low implementation burden, care disruption, measurable outcomes | Whether the organization publicly prioritizes quality, access, or operational change. |
| Finance / procurement | cost, risk, vendor/process fit | Usually not visible from public research; confirm only in an authorized conversation. |

## Market-language and category map

| Public category language | How adjacent sellers frame it | MindfulText implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workforce resilience, retention, burnout, staff wellbeing, workforce development, operational capacity | [MTM Services](https://www.thenationalcouncil.org/event/workforce-development-training-and-consultation-program/) sells tailored workforce-development consulting around retention, burnout, and patient satisfaction. | Do not compete as generic training; position any future test around a low-lift daily support layer. |
| Documentation burden, clinician efficiency, burnout reduction | [Eleos Health](https://www.thenationalcouncil.org/eleos-health-partner/) sells behavioral-health documentation automation tied to reduced administrative burden. | MindfulText does not remove documentation work; its claim must be distinct and modest. |
| Trauma-responsive culture, staff sustainability, organizational intervention | [Five Ives](https://www.fiveives.com/) positions a system-level workforce-sustainability service for high-pressure organizations. | Reinforce that MindfulText complements structural/cultural interventions rather than replacing them. |
| Employee wellness and workday reset | `EV-001` provides founder-supplied internal evidence that short daily texts can be valued in a care-delivery context. | This is the closest existing proof of product fit, but it needs rights confirmation before any external attribution or statistic. |

## Recommendation

Create `GH-004 — Low-disruption workforce reset for community behavioral-health
providers` as the first atomic hypothesis under `SEG-002`. It passes the
research-admission threshold because the category has documented pain,
evidence, reachable public account contexts, and a plausible fit; its
willingness-to-pay remains unproven. The next Run 003 packet should be chosen
only after Mark reviews whether one of `ACC-001`–`ACC-005` is the right
research focus and whether the claim is sufficiently narrow.
