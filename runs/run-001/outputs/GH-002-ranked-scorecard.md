## Recommended order of learning

1. **Qualify the organization buyer:** confirm a non-MHA provider’s LCSW cohort, named workforce/clinical owner, and stress/retention/quality priority.
2. **Validate the strongest existing signal:** import a privacy-safe summary of the Dutchess case study/surveys and determine what actually changed, for whom, and under what program conditions.
3. **Test the direct user alternative:** only then compare a stress-first LCSW proposition with one adjacent, separately measured outcome proposition.

## Disconfirming evidence applied

- Social-work mindfulness studies are not all uniformly positive on stress/anxiety outcomes, and key direct studies are small or non-U.S.
- SAMHSA cautions that organizational conditions drive burnout; an individual practice cannot substitute for workload, staffing, supervision, or culture changes.
- Direct public research establishes neither organization budget nor LCSW willingness to pay.
