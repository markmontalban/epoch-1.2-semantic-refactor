# GH-002 — U.S. TAM Note: Elevated-stress LCSWs

Last updated: 2026-07-12 23:54:25 PDT — edited by: Codex

**Status:** `agent-hypothesis` — TAM is scale context, not willingness-to-pay proof, validation, or a forecast.

## ICP eligibility

**Role / group:** U.S. clinical social workers, used as the closest national proxy for LCSWs/LICSWs/LISWs.
**Observable behavior / current alternative:** Self-identify as experiencing elevated occupational stress in client-facing work and may be using no program, generic wellness content, individual self-care, supervision, EAP support, or another alternative.
**Situation / constraint:** A nonclinical, stress-first MindfulText program must complement—not replace—workload, staffing, supervision, clinical care, crisis support, or organization-level burnout interventions.

This is sufficiently defined for a preliminary U.S. TAM range. It is not sufficiently defined for SAM or SOM: no reachable subset, program eligibility filter, price decision, or credible go-to-market capacity has been selected.

## Count and formula

[ASWB’s 2024 workforce study](https://www.aswb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Social-Work-Workforce-Study-Series-Report-2.pdf) estimates **272,652 Clinical social workers** in the United States (58.87% of 463,112 estimated licensed social workers). Use that as the count proxy, with the caveat that state credential labels differ and licensure data have multi-state-license limitations.

`eligible clinical-social-work proxy × assumed elevated-stress share × illustrative annual per-active-user price = TAM`

| Scenario | Eligible share assumption | Annual price assumption | Calculation | TAM |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Low | 10% | $60 | 272,652 × 10% × $60 | **$1.64M** |
| Base | 25% | $120 | 272,652 × 25% × $120 | **$8.18M** |
| High | 40% | $240 | 272,652 × 40% × $240 | **$26.17M** |

## Assumptions and limits

- The 10% / 25% / 40% shares are sensitivity assumptions, **not** a claimed national elevated-stress prevalence rate for LCSWs.
- The $60 / $120 / $240 annual prices are illustrative per-active-user values, **not** observed MindfulText pricing or evidence of willingness to pay.
- The count may include credential categories that do not exactly match the label “LCSW” and excludes unlicensed social workers; it is a transparent proxy, not a census of every potential user.
- The range includes individual and organization-sponsored use; it does not identify who pays, what share is addressable through channels, or how much implementation/support would cost.
- No SAM/SOM is calculated because a narrower number would not yet change the immediate decision: first qualify one buyer path and verify the existing customer evidence.
