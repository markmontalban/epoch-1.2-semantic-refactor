## [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-002--mindfultext-for-elevated-stress-lcsws|GH-002]] — working brief

**Primary audience:** [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-018]] — U.S. LCSWs who self-identify as experiencing elevated work stress in client-facing clinical work. **Buyer distinction:** the LCSW may buy individually; [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-019]] non-MHA community behavioral-health organizations are a separate potential sponsor/buyer; [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-020]] LCSW communities are a separate channel.

**Working proposition:** **agent-hypothesis** — a MindfulText-delivered, nonclinical “LCSW Stress Reset” program may support brief stress-regulation practice and lower perceived stress or emotional exhaustion. It should complement, never substitute for, organizational workload, staffing, supervision, clinical care, or crisis support.

**Founder signal:** **founder-assumption** — Mental Health America of Dutchess County has reportedly paid for MindfulText for more than two years, with a case study and surveys reporting lower stress and organizational outcomes. The source assets are not in this run, so this is a lead to verify—not transferable efficacy or willingness-to-pay proof.

## Candidate angles and ranking logic

1. **Organization-sponsored LCSW Stress Reset** — the lead path because it pairs a high-pain setting with a potential budget owner, while explicitly keeping organizational interventions in the design.
2. **Individual LCSW post-session reset** — a sharper user proposition with less procurement friction, but no direct price signal yet.
3. **Association/community cohort** — an efficient LCSW channel, but a channel is not automatically a paying employer.
4. **FQHC integrated-behavioral-health cohort** — concrete LCSW staffing proxy, but LCSWs are a multidisciplinary subset and ownership must be qualified.
5. **Outcome-menu expansion** — only after the stress program is clear; it tests one extra, measurable capability rather than promising the entire screenshot.

## Evidence gaps and proposed tests

- **Highest-leverage gap — buyer ownership:** Which non-MHA behavioral-health employer has a public LCSW cohort plus a named workforce/clinical owner who treats stress, retention, or quality as a priority? No outreach occurred in this run.
- **Highest-leverage gap — transferable outcome proof:** Can the Dutchess case study and surveys be imported in a privacy-safe form, spot-checked, and tied to comparable LCSW participation and a defined outcome measure?
- **Top test 1 — organization path:** If Mark later authorizes manual outreach, test a tightly scoped pilot-design conversation with a qualified [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-019]] leader. Advance only on a level-2 buyer-intent signal; do not treat a directory listing as interest.
- **Top test 2 — individual path:** If Mark later authorizes a manual, consent-based test, compare a stress-first LCSW concept with one adjacent outcome concept and measure qualified resonance, repeat engagement, and willingness to pay separately.

**Next unanswered question:** Is the buyer willing to sponsor a short LCSW stress-support program alongside organizational actions, rather than expecting an individual mindfulness tool to solve structural burnout?
