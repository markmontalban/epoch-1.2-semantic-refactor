# PB-CORE-001 — MindfulText sales-playbook core

## Shared position

MindfulText may be explored as a voluntary, asynchronous, nonclinical
mindfulness practice. It does not replace structural workplace support,
therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis response, supervision, or care delivery.

## Shared discovery contract

- Confirm the problem owner, user, safe use moment, existing alternatives,
  required reviewers, proof threshold, and disqualifier.
- Treat public initiatives and role titles as qualification cues, not intent.
- Ask what would make the offer additive, redundant, unsafe, or irrelevant.

## Shared evidence and action boundaries

- Label facts, founder assumptions, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Do not claim stress, burnout, retention, quality, performance, or financial
  outcomes without direct applicable evidence.
- Pricing, outreach, contact collection, publication, pilots, and automation
  require separate approval.
