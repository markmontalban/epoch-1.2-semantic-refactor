# FQHC integrated behavioral-health overlay

Apply after [[playbooks/PLAYBOOK-CORE|PB-CORE-001]].

- **Context delta:** primary-care integration, multidisciplinary staffing,
  access, grants, compliance, and health-center governance may alter ownership.
- **Role delta:** keep behavioral-health, workforce/learning, clinical,
  operations, finance/grants, privacy, and IT functions separate until verified.
- **Proof delta:** qualify whether staff support belongs at the health-center,
  service-line, partner, or individual level.
- **Disqualifier:** the route assumes FQHC and CCBHC buying paths are interchangeable.
