# Community behavioral-health overlay

Apply after [[playbooks/PLAYBOOK-CORE|PB-CORE-001]].

- **Context delta:** high-acuity, staffing, retention, access, coordination,
  quality, and reporting constraints shape the workday.
- **Position delta:** a small voluntary staff-support layer beside structural
  workforce work; never inside treatment, crisis response, or required reporting.
- **Proof delta:** demonstrate workflow fit, accessibility, voluntary use, and
  low implementation burden before proposing an outcome test.
- **Disqualifier:** the route requires clinical integration, mandatory use,
  patient data, crisis coverage, or a promised workforce result.
