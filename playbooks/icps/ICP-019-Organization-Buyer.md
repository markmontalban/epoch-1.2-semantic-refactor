# Organization-side workforce buyer overlay

Apply after the shared core and selected segment overlay.

- **Buyer delta:** investigate executive, workforce/HR, clinical/program,
  quality/operations, finance/procurement, privacy/compliance, and IT functions;
  do not collapse them into one buyer.
- **Discovery delta:** identify who owns the problem, who validates safe use,
  who can stop the proposal, and what existing support makes it redundant.
- **Evidence delta:** organization-level public context can qualify research
  attention but cannot confirm authority, budget, timing, or interest.
