**icp_id:** [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-020]]
**buyer:** **agent-hypothesis** — executive, membership, continuing-education, program, or conference leader at an LCSW association or peer community.
**user:** LCSW members or community participants with self-identified elevated work stress.
**vertical_context:** Professional association, peer community, or continuing-education-adjacent cohort.
**pain:** A community leader may seek a credible member-wellbeing benefit; individual member stress is the underlying user pain.
**angle:** A facilitated or time-bounded MindfulText cohort framed around stress support, reflection, and professional sustainability.
**gtm_strategy:** Channel-led cohort proposal that keeps the association’s buyer role separate from an employer’s staff-wellbeing budget.
**channel:** [Clinical Social Work Association](https://www.clinicalsocialworkassociation.org/about) and [NASW chapter network](https://www.socialworkers.org/about/chapters/find).
**offer_hypothesis:** A member program that provides a simple, measured LCSW stress-support experience without asserting CE credit, clinical treatment, or association-wide efficacy.
**evidence:** **evidence** — social-worker mindfulness studies justify a narrow stress-support test; **agent-hypothesis** — a community can convene a cohort or sponsor a member benefit.
**uncertainty:** Whether an association has budget authority, a member demand signal, a suitable delivery model, or a reason to prefer MindfulText over existing benefits/content.
**target_signal:** Level 2 — an association/program leader discusses a funded cohort, a member-benefit pilot, or an introduction to an eligible sponsor.
**cheapest_test:** If later authorized by Mark, qualify one association’s program ownership and member-fit before proposing a pilot; do not mistake audience reach for purchase intent.
**rubric_score:** Pain 1/2; Evidence 1/2; Buyer reachability 1/2; Willingness to pay 0/2; MindfulText fit & effort 1/2; **total 4/10**.
**review_date:** 2026-07-14

## Score rationale

This route concentrates the audience but has no present proof that the channel
will buy or distribute a program. Its value is a qualified member cohort, not
an assumed employer sale.
