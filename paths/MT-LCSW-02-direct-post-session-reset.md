# Path — Direct LCSW Post-session Reset

Last updated: 2026-07-12 23:54:25 PDT — edited by: Codex

**path_id:** MT-LCSW-02
**status:** candidate
**hypothesis_id:** GH-002
**icp_id:** ICP-018
**buyer:** Individual LCSW.
**user:** The same individual LCSW who self-identifies as experiencing elevated occupational stress.
**vertical_context:** Client-facing clinical social-work practice, including an optional between-session or end-of-day context.
**pain:** **agent-hypothesis** — an LCSW may want a brief, private, nonclinical practice after emotionally demanding client work, but not a generic wellness library.
**angle:** “A brief reset for the work you carry after a hard session,” tested as a stress-first proposition rather than a clinical-treatment or performance claim.
**gtm_strategy:** Direct concept/engagement test with a clearly defined clinical-social-work audience; associations are a possible channel, not buyer proof.
**channel:** [Clinical Social Work Association affiliated societies](https://www.clinicalsocialworkassociation.org/CSWA-AffiliatedSocieties) and [NASW chapters](https://www.socialworkers.org/about/chapters/find), subject to Mark-approved, consent-based use.
**offer_hypothesis:** An individual MindfulText LCSW Stress Reset sequence that supports stress regulation, self-compassion, and a short reflective practice without handling client material.
**evidence:** **evidence** — direct social-work trials provide enough basis to test stress/emotional-exhaustion and self-compassion measures; **agent-hypothesis** — the post-session moment will be more compelling than a broad mindfulness offer.
**uncertainty:** Whether LCSWs choose this moment, repeat engagement, willingness to self-pay, and whether an existing alternative already meets the need.
**target_signal:** Level 1 — qualified LCSWs identify the stress-first concept as relevant and engage with a voluntary, consent-based prototype; WTP remains a separate question.
**cheapest_test:** If Mark later authorizes a manual test, compare the stress-first proposition with one adjacent outcome proposition and collect only privacy-safe, consented feedback. No test or outreach was performed by this run.
**rubric_score:** Pain 2/2; Evidence 1/2; Buyer reachability 1/2; Willingness to pay 0/2; MindfulText fit & effort 1/2; **total 5/10**.
