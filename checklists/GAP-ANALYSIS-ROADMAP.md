## C. Feature backlog

- [ ] `C1` **Create buyer-path and budget map** — For one selected branch,
  map sponsor, user, buying group, possible budget owner, trigger, alternatives,
  and explicit unknowns. Use only current public evidence until a separately
  approved conversation exists; this is the highest-priority input to `E4`.
- [ ] `C2` **Map alternatives and differentiation** — Compare MindfulText with
  EAPs, wellness programs, training, apps, and structural workforce responses;
  define where the product is complementary, redundant, or inappropriate. This
  must not promise retention, clinical, or organizational outcomes.
- [ ] `C3` **Design a minimum pilot** — If `E4` supports a market test, define
  the smallest cohort, duration, activation measure, buyer-valued outcome,
  stop condition, and "no meaningful improvement" rule before `E5`–`E6`.
- [ ] `C4` **Prepare a trust answer set** — Draft the internal answers on data
  handling, privacy, security, accessibility, implementation load, clinical
  boundary, and procurement constraints that a qualified buyer would need to
  assess. It is not external collateral until separately approved.
- [ ] `C5` **Plan capability and capital milestones** — After a Level 2 buyer
  intent signal, identify the next de-risking milestone, needed advisor or
  operating capability, capital use, and evidence for any investor narrative.
  Do not force SAM/SOM, exit, IP, or fundraising work before it changes a
  decision.

## D. Roadmap and gates

| Stage | Work | Entry condition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Now — internal de-risking | `C1`, `C2` | Mark selects a segment/branch for the next bounded work. | A more falsifiable `E4` market-test decision. |
| If market test is supported | `C3`, `C4` | `E4` says the evidence warrants a manual test. | A reviewable `E5`–`E6` test packet, not automatic outreach. |
