## DR-47 — W4a-L1a recipient presentation and invitation disclosure

**Prototype amendment:** [[decisions/2026-08-14-opaque-invitation-attribution-prototype|DR-49]] retains the opaque recipient-link mechanics in this record but supersedes its visitor-facing invitation-disclosure copy for the prototype.

Mark approved the recommended W4a-L1a direction on 2026-08-14. The branded recipient experience keeps the existing cautious staff-support proposition and uses one basic CTA into the later anonymous fit reflection. Private invitation-level attribution remains behind an opaque token; the prototype does not disclose that attribution to visitors.

### Approved presentation

> **For community behavioral-health care teams**
>
> # A brief, optional staff-support layer for care teams.
>
> MindfulText offers short mindfulness prompts by text for voluntary use in
> small non-care moments. It can complement existing workforce, learning, or
> wellbeing support. It is not clinical care or a solution to staffing,
> workload, pay, supervision, or retention.
>
> **Explore your staff-support fit**
>
> A short, anonymous reflection will help you consider whether the conditions
> exist for an appropriate staff-support pilot. You will see useful,
> non-scored guidance before deciding whether to identify yourself or request
> a conversation.

### Historical invitation disclosure — superseded for the prototype

The following was originally approved for placement near the CTA. It is retained as provenance only and must not be used in prototype copy under DR-49:

> **About this invitation link**
>
> This link was created for the original recipient of a MindfulText
> invitation. Activity on the link may be associated with that invitation,
> but links can be forwarded, so we do not assume we know who is visiting.
>
> If you choose the fit reflection, your answers remain anonymous unless you
> explicitly choose to connect an approved result or summary to your identity
> after seeing it.

Invalid, paused, revoked, or expired links must later receive generic safe
handling rather than recipient-specific wording.

### Proof and measurement boundary

- Do not use MHA Dutchess proof in the first version. A later restrained proof
  module requires verification of the exact cleared quote, figures, dates,
  denominator, attribution, logo permission, and interpretation.
- PostHog may later receive only approved anonymous milestones and
  non-personal variant properties. Voluntary identity association belongs in
  Growth Ops, not PostHog.
- Do not place the LinkedIn Insight Tag on the L2 reflection or results path.
  Any later aggregate LinkedIn reporting remains deferred behind its recorded
  gate and separate approval.
- Formbricks remains undecided pending the W4a-L2b feasibility, privacy, and
  data-boundary comparison.

**Later selection:** [[decisions/2026-08-14-w4a-l2b-custom-reflection-direction|DR-50]] selects a small custom, Formbricks-inspired fixed-choice reflection. This historical feasibility note does not select a Formbricks provider.

## Consequences
