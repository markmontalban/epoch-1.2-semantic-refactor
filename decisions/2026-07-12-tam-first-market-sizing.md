## DR-27 — TAM-first market sizing

An agent calculates a U.S. TAM only after it can state a clear ICP as a role or
group, observable current behavior/alternative, and the situation or constraint
that makes the Pain relevant. TAM is a transparent annual-revenue range—not a
single false-precision number—with this structure:

`eligible U.S. ICP count × annual pricing assumption = TAM`

The note cites its count and pricing sources, date, inclusions/exclusions,
low/base/high assumptions, and material unknowns. It estimates potential annual
MindfulText revenue at that pricing assumption—not total category spend. It is labeled
`agent-hypothesis` until independently checked. Market size informs the
decision to spend more research time; it is not a willingness-to-pay signal or
a substitute for customer feedback.

Start with **TAM in the United States**. Do not automatically calculate SAM or
SOM. Add SAM only when product scope, reachable channel, geography, or a real
constraint makes it a decision-useful subset; add SOM only after a credible
timeframe and go-to-market capacity assumption exist. State why that deeper
step is needed before doing it.
