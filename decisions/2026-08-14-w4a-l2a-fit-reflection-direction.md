### Approved direction

- Use fixed-choice questions about the workday moment, possible mindfulness
  use, accessibility, staff dignity and choice, organizational responsibility,
  and one pilot learning question.
- Return non-scored guidance before any identity request: promising moment,
  possible use, respectful access, leadership ownership, one useful pilot
  question, and an optional two-minute example.
- Describe MindfulText as brief, optional mindfulness prompts by text that are
  easy to introduce around a team's workday. Content may align to the chosen
  moment and organization language; do not promise bespoke delivery or an
  organization outcome.
- Reserve "evidence-informed" for a later validated claim. Do not ask about
  budget or procurement in the reflection.

### Boundary

This decision authorizes direction and documentation reconciliation only. It
does not authorize code, public data collection, provider or analytics
activation, credentials, email automation, outreach, deployment, Production,
or identity association. Budget/procurement discussion belongs only in a
separately approved, voluntary identified follow-up path.
