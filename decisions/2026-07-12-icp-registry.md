## DR-28 — ICP registry and hypothesis linkage

`growth/ICP-REGISTRY.md` is the canonical catalogue of buyer ICPs and related
channel, partner, and research audiences. It uses stable non-semantic
`ICP-###` identifiers. A number identifies an audience record; it does not
encode vertical, quality, priority, or the truth of a market claim.

Each `GH-###` uses one primary `ICP ID` when its audience is known. The
identifier remains blank only while the starting thought is too incomplete to
match an existing segment. An agent may add a candidate ICP when needed, but
labels it `agent-hypothesis` until privacy-safe evidence or cited public
research strengthens, weakens, or retires it.

The registry distinguishes a prospective buyer from a channel/partner or
funder/research audience. A GTM approach is evaluated only within its linked
ICP and hypothesis context: success with one audience does not validate it for
another. Tests, path cards, and signals retain the relevant ICP ID so later
matrix comparisons do not overwrite the original context.

Initial entries consolidate Mark's supplied testing notes. They are concise
founder-reported history, contain no contact details, and do not constitute
customer evidence, validation, or outbound authorization.
