## DR-33 — RUN-005 care-delivery organization registry

**Decision:** Mark approved `RUN-005`, an initial, public-research-only,
four-hour lab-validation run for `SEG-001 — Care-delivery organizations`.
The run will identify and normalize 50–75 U.S. care-delivery organizations,
compare relevant subsegments including `SEG-002` and `SEG-003`, and retain a
durable public organization registry for later reviewed research.

**Registry boundary:** The registry holds organizations, stable `ACC-###`
identifiers, organization and segment classifications, public scale/workforce
context, plausible purchasing functions and titles, source references,
confidence, and explicit unknowns. It does not hold personal names, emails,
phone numbers, inferred individual authority, or contact-enrichment data.
Plausible buyer functions are research hypotheses, not evidence of a budget,
readiness, authority, willingness to pay, or buying intent.

**Evidence and action boundary:** Codex may use public official organization,
government, and sector-body sources. Each retained organization requires at
least one direct public source; priority records receive a second source when
available. The run may not scrape, monitor, use a paid database, log in,
collect personal contact details, send a message, publish, create a CRM, or
authorize a buyer conversation, campaign, partnership, or market test.

**Retention boundary:** Canonical data remains in a repository-local,
source-normalized account-registry folder with a schema/retention guide and
separate organization and source tables. The human-readable account-intelligence
summary may link selected findings but must not duplicate the entire registry.

**Execution result:** Closed within the four-hour ceiling with 56 normalized
organization records, a 16-source public ledger, and a buyer-function/title
taxonomy. Six records retain additional public workforce/operating context;
the remaining directory-backed records explicitly retain their first-party
verification gap. No external action occurred.

**Status:** Mark-approved and executed 2026-07-15. This decision extends
`DR-31` and preserves `DR-29` and `DR-32` public-research and no-outbound
boundaries.
