## DR-35 — Gap-analysis roadmap and feature backlog

**Decision:** Mark directed creation of a durable, proposal-only
gap-analysis roadmap and feature backlog for Epoch 1. It adapts the
FourthWave assessment method into MindfulText-specific market, product,
adoption, trust, and later capital/team readiness lenses.

**Rationale:** Epoch 1 already has a path-ranking scorecard and a gated
customer-development plan, but no single durable place to show the major
company-level unknowns, their smallest proof, and the gate that each informs.
The source method is useful for honest de-risking; its AgTech-specific
regulatory, IP, exit, and funding criteria are not copied as immediate
MindfulText requirements.

**Scope:** The new [Gap-Analysis Roadmap & Feature Backlog](../checklists/GAP-ANALYSIS-ROADMAP.md)
records five proposed capabilities: buyer-path and budget mapping,
alternatives/differentiation mapping, minimum-pilot design, a
trust/procurement answer set, and later capability/capital milestone planning.
It links each to the appropriate existing `E3`–`F4` gate.

**Authority and boundary:** The Master Checklist remains the execution
authority. A backlog item is not approval to build, contact anyone, conduct a
market test, spend money, change data handling, make product claims, or change
the approved Growth Scorecard. Any new run, manual conversation, or external
artifact still requires the approval already required by the charter and
applicable decision record.

**Affected documents:** `../checklists/GAP-ANALYSIS-ROADMAP.md`,
`../MASTER-CHECKLIST.md`, `../CONTEXT-MAP.md`, `../README.md`,
`DECISION-REGISTER.md`, and root `AGENTS.md`.

**Status:** Mark-directed and recorded 2026-07-15. The roadmap record is
complete; every backlog item remains proposal-only until its named gate opens.
