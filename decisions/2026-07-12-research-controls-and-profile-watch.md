## DR-25 — Research controls and public-profile watch

Each `GH-###` in `growth/HYPOTHESES.md` has two optional settings:

- `Research: auto` (or blank) means the manually selected agent independently
  chooses, researches, and cites relevant public sources without waiting for
  more founder input. A compact research line can instead set a model
  preference, sources/communities, question, and fields or score dimensions to
  update. The manually named Run 001 provider remains authoritative; a model
  preference does not add a provider surface.
- `Profile tracking: off | discover | watch` controls public-profile research
  for that hypothesis. `off` retains no profiles; `discover` proposes public
  candidates in run notes; `watch` creates minimal, hypothesis-linked entries
  in `growth/PUBLIC-PROFILE-WATCH.md`.

The public-profile watch stores only a public URL, public role/context, and
concise relevance/signal summary. It is manual research, not automated
monitoring, scraping, contact discovery, a CRM, or outreach. It must never
retain private contact details, sensitive traits, or raw personal messages.

Public findings can inform Pain, Evidence strength, or Buyer reachability in a
path score; they do not independently establish willingness to pay or validate
the hypothesis. Customer feedback remains the validation source.
