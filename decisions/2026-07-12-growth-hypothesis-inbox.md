## DR-23 — Growth hypothesis inbox and agent-assisted exploration

`growth/HYPOTHESES.md` becomes the single **Growth Hypothesis Inbox**. Mark
may enter one sentence, a partial thought, or any subset of Product, ICP, Pain,
Signals, and Offer-Outcome. Product defaults to MindfulText; a named
MindfulText sub-offering, prototype, or MVP is a valid variation.

The selected agent may turn an inbox item into a structured working hypothesis:
infer missing fields, research public sources, identify alternatives and
contradictory evidence, suggest a cheapest validation test, and draft path
cards. Public research must cite its sources and cannot use logins, private
data, outbound messages, or unapproved web sources that require access.

All agent-filled material begins as `agent-hypothesis`, not proof. Real
customer feedback, imported research, or direct signal can strengthen,
weaken, park, or kill a hypothesis. A separate signal log is optional; add a
central log only when multiple incoming signals justify it.

**Status:** Mark-approved 2026-07-12. This supersedes the detailed source /
owner / rights / data-class intake fields for the prototype learning loop.
