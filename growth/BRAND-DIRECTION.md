# Growth — Brand Direction

Last updated: 2026-07-28 10:44:41 PDT — edited by: Codex

`BRD-###` records the durable brand foundation that should inform later
positioning, product choices, growth hypotheses, GTM motions, and public
communication. A BRD record is a founder-direction document, not evidence of
audience resonance, demand, efficacy, or market validation.

## Record contract

Each `BRD-###` record should preserve:

- vision and mission;
- worldview or point of view;
- brand promise and first-audience framing;
- voice, tone, and non-negotiable boundaries;
- positioning pillars, tensions, and open decisions; and
- links to the `SOC`, `GH`, `PRO`, `GTM`, `ICP`, or `RUN` records it informs.

## BRD-001 — MindfulText Brand Foundation

**Status:** Provisional founder direction; not externally validated.

**Purpose:** Establish the underlying identity and positioning spine for
MindfulText before choosing specific acquisition motions or product bets.

**Current inputs:** [[growth/SOCIAL-IDEA-LEDGER|SOC-006]] (breadth versus focus), [[growth/SOCIAL-IDEA-LEDGER|SOC-008]] (mindfulness,
comedy, and constructive ordinary good), [[growth/SOCIAL-IDEA-LEDGER|SOC-009]] (mindfulness-rooted Social Studio), [[runs/run-011/RUN-011-Comedy-Concept|RUN-011]] (internal founder-theme
positioning packet), [[runs/run-018/RUN-018-Mindfulness-Social-Studio|RUN-018]] (channel and human-agent operating model), and [[decisions/2026-07-16-social-intelligence-founder-content-direction#dr-36--social-intelligence-and-founder-content-exploration-direction|DR-36]].

**Working direction:** MindfulText helps people make brief, private,
non-clinical mindfulness part of ordinary life and work. Its worldview is that
mindfulness helps people notice what fast systems make them miss, then choose a
more human response. The brand should make constructive good feel approachable
rather than moralizing, while preserving room for humor, lived experience, and
multiple industries. Technology, founder life, care work, and film are lenses
for concrete stories—not separate brand promises.

**First positioning question:** What first audience promise makes this
worldview understandable enough to follow, while keeping the broader mission
intact? The provisional founder-story answer is people doing ambitious,
high-pressure work who want progress without losing attention, agency, or care;
it requires a separately approved baseline before it is treated as resonance or
market evidence.

**Boundaries:** This record does not claim public resonance, therapeutic
benefit, clinical efficacy, buyer intent, or permission to publish, monitor,
contact, or spend. Those require their own evidence and approvals.

**Downstream use:** Link this record from any later `GH-###`, `PRO-###`,
`GTM-###`, or content/theme record that adopts or tests part of this direction.
