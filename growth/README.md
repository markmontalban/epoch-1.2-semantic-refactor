## Supporting records

- [ICP Registry](ICP-REGISTRY.md) — stable audience, buyer, and channel IDs.
- [Evidence Register](EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md) — concise, permission-aware evidence references that change prioritization.
- [Account Intelligence](ACCOUNT-INTELLIGENCE.md) — public-only account research generated by a bounded approved run; not a CRM or monitoring system.
- [Care-delivery Account Registry](account-registry/README.md) — source-normalized, organization-level `ACC-###` records; preserve evidence history and explicit unknowns here.
- [GTM Motions](GTM-MOTIONS.md) — reusable research and validation approaches, distinct from segments and hypotheses.
- [Signals](SIGNALS.md) — shared cross-hypothesis evidence only; add a short
  signal beneath its hypothesis first.
- [Scorecard](SCORECARD.md) — approved path-ranking rubric.
- [Public Profile Watch](PUBLIC-PROFILE-WATCH.md) — optional, public-only
  profile research when a hypothesis explicitly enables it.
- [Social Idea Ledger](SOCIAL-IDEA-LEDGER.md) — low-friction social idea and
  saved-example capture; an idea is not evidence or a run.
