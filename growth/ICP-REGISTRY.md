# Growth — ICP Registry

Last updated: 2026-07-13 00:23:25 PDT — edited by: Codex

This is the canonical list of MindfulText audiences. Use its stable `ICP-###`
identifier in a Growth Hypothesis whenever the intended audience is known.
The number identifies the audience record, not a rank, vertical, or evidence
verdict; do not reuse it if the wording later becomes more precise.

## How to use the registry

- **Buyer ICP** is an organization or person that could adopt or pay for
  MindfulText. **Channel / partner** and **funder / research** records are
  valuable routes to market or learning, but are not interchangeable with a
  buyer.
- A `GH-###` links one primary ICP ID. Create another hypothesis variant to
  test the same angle or GTM strategy with another audience.
- `Recorded test` and `Access-led` below are concise founder-supplied history,
  not independently verified evidence or a conclusion that the segment works.
  `Candidate` is an `agent-hypothesis` until a signal or research supports it.
- Keep contact names, raw conversations, and other sensitive material out of
  this registry. Link only a privacy-safe signal or source reference when one
  changes a segment's status.

## Adding or changing an ICP

1. Assign the next unused `ICP-###` ID; never encode the vertical in the ID.
2. State one audience, its role (`Buyer ICP`, `Channel / partner`, or `Funder / research`), and the situation in which MindfulText could matter.
3. Label it `Candidate` until a privacy-safe signal or cited research changes the status. Record any outcome in `SIGNALS.md` and link the relevant `GH-###`.
4. If an audience definition materially changes, retain the old entry as
   `Archived / split` with a pointer to the replacement rather than silently
   changing the meaning of past hypotheses.
