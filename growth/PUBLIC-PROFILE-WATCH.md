## Profiles

### PP-001 — CCBHC organization pool

- Profile: Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics and designated collaborating organizations
- URL: https://www.samhsa.gov/communities/certified-community-behavioral-health-clinics
- Linked hypothesis: GH-002
- Public role/context: Non-MHA behavioral-health provider pool; public decision-maker roles to observe are CEO / executive director, chief clinical officer or VP clinical, workforce/HR leader, and quality/outcomes leader.
- Relevance to the hypothesis: Candidate organization buyers (ICP-019) with a high-acuity behavioral-health context; certify an organization’s LCSW staffing and staff-wellbeing owner before treating it as qualified.
- Score contribution: Buyer reachability
- Public signals worth revisiting: CCBHC designation/expansion, LCSW or behavioral-health job postings, workforce/retention initiatives, strategic plans, and public quality/outcome reports.
- Last checked: 2026-07-12
- Status: watching
