# Care-delivery buyer-function and title taxonomy

Last updated: 2026-07-15 00:40:01 PDT — edited by: Codex

This taxonomy supports organization-level targeting research. A listed title
is a role to validate in public research or a separately approved conversation;
it is not a named person, a contact target, or proof of authority, budget, or
purchase intent.

## BGP-CCBHC-001 — Community behavioral-health / CCBHC organizations

| Buying-group function | Titles to look for | Why it may be relevant | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | Chief Executive Officer; Executive Director; President; Chief Operating Officer | May own organization-wide workforce, service-delivery, or strategic priorities. | Whether staff support is a priority, who owns it, and whether a budget exists. |
| Clinical/program sponsor | Chief Clinical Officer; Chief Medical Officer; Vice President of Clinical Services; Clinical Director; Director of Behavioral Health | May own clinical workforce experience, program integration, and care-delivery workflow. | Whether a nonclinical support program fits scope, policy, and workflow. |
| Workforce sponsor | Chief Human Resources Officer; Vice President of People/HR; Director of Human Resources; Vice President of Workforce Development; Learning and Development Director | May own employee wellbeing, retention, learning, or benefits processes. | Whether staff support is distinct from existing benefits, EAP, supervision, workload, or compensation work. |
| Quality/operations sponsor | Chief Quality Officer; VP/Director of Quality; VP/Director of Operations; Practice/Service-Line Director | May see workflow strain, operational constraints, and implementation feasibility. | Whether this role is part of a purchase path or only an evaluator. |
| Financial/procurement approver | Chief Financial Officer; VP Finance; Director of Procurement; Supply Chain; Contracts; Grants/Finance Director | May participate in funding, contract, or procurement review. | Funding source, threshold, government rules, and whether this is centralized. |
| Risk/technical reviewer | Chief Information Officer; IT Director; Privacy Officer; Compliance Officer; Security Officer | May review data handling, vendor risk, integration, accessibility, and policy. | Whether a low-data, nonclinical offering triggers formal review and when. |

Public leadership pages in this research publish examples of many of these
titles, including CEO, COO, CFO, CHRO, CIO, CMO, compliance, legal, strategy,
clinical, and service-line roles. Those observations support the taxonomy;
they do not make any listed role a buyer or contact target. See `SRC-012`–
`SRC-015` in the [source ledger](sources.csv).

## BGP-FQHC-001 — Integrated primary-care / FQHC comparison organizations

| Buying-group function | Titles to look for | Why it may be relevant | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive and medical sponsor | Chief Executive Officer; Chief Medical Officer; Chief Operating Officer; VP Medical Services | May own care model and resource allocation for primary-care delivery. | Whether a staff-support use case is relevant, funded, and distinct from patient care. |
| Integration/clinical sponsor | VP/Director of Behavioral Health Integration; Behavioral Health Director; Integrated Care Director; Medical Director | May own the relationship between behavioral-health and primary-care workflows. | Whether the role owns a workforce initiative or only patient-care design. |
| Workforce/learning sponsor | CHRO; VP People; Workforce Development; Learning and Development; Employee Health/Wellbeing Director | May own employee support, learning, or benefits processes. | Whether external support is in scope and who would approve it. |
| Financial, grants, and risk reviewer | CFO; VP Finance; VP Grants and Research; Procurement; Compliance; Privacy; IT | May participate in funding, vendor review, privacy, and contracting. | Funding source, formal procurement threshold, and required technical review. |

El Rio’s public leadership page provides a comparison example of Behavioral
Health and Integration Services, Medical Services, and Grants and Research
titles (`SRC-016`). It does not establish any buying role or a fit for
MindfulText.

## Use rules

- Start with the function, not a person. Capture a public title only where the
  organization itself publishes it and only in a later, explicitly approved
  research scope that needs it.
- The likely first internal champion may be a workforce, learning, clinical,
  quality, or operations leader. That possibility is an `agent-hypothesis`,
  not a conclusion.
- MindfulText must be framed, if at all, as an optional nonclinical,
  low-disruption support complement. Do not claim it solves staffing,
  compensation, workload, supervision, retention, clinical outcomes, or
  care-quality problems.
