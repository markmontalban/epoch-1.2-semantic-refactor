# MindfulText — Hypothesis Test Board

**Owner:** [[growth/HYPOTHESES|Growth Hypotheses]] — this board organizes the
test queue; each `GH-###` record remains the canonical definition and evidence
owner. **Execution status:** [[MASTER-CHECKLIST|Master Checklist]]. **Approval
status:** the linked dated `DR-###` record.

Use this as the shared, compact handoff surface for people or agents deciding
what to test next. It does not authorize research, outreach, spending,
publishing, or a new run.

## Operating rules

- Keep one row per atomic belief. Split a broad belief before designing a test.
- Record an update under the matching card as: `YYYY-MM-DD — agent/person —
  finding | evidence link | proposed next move`. Label unsupported conclusions
  `agent-hypothesis`; do not overwrite the canonical `GH` fields without
  evidence or founder direction.
- A completed task changes only its own evidence question. It does not prove
  demand, efficacy, willingness to pay, or authorization for external action.
- When a test needs bounded execution, draft a `RUN-###` card; only Mark can
  approve execution. Preserve competing evidence and disconfirmers.

## Test cards

### [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-004--low-disruption-workforce-reset-for-community-behavioral-health-providers|GH-004]] — Community behavioral-health workforce reset

**Decision:** Is there enough account-specific buyer and workflow evidence to
justify a bounded pilot conversation?

**Pass / fail signal:** pass only if one account has a visible relevant
workforce context, a plausible buyer function, and no material contradiction;
otherwise park or choose another account. This is qualification, not buyer
intent.

**Agent updates:**

### [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-005--reflective-onboarding-to-a-personalized-mindfultext-rhythm|GH-005]] — Reflective onboarding

**Decision:** Does a transparent, nonclinical reflection help a prospect begin
and return to a MindfulText practice?

**Pass / fail signal:** predefine improvement versus the generic path for one
activation metric; treat neutral or lower completion/return as a failure or
inconclusive result, not as proof of personalization value.

**Agent updates:**

- `RUN-016` supports an internal prototype decision only; it did not test
  customer value, conversion, retention, or willingness to pay.

### [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-003--retention-gift--mindfulness-desk-object-for-paid-subscribers|GH-003]] — Retention gift

**Decision:** Can a useful, distinctive physical gift create incremental paid
value that exceeds landed cost and operational burden?

**Pass / fail signal:** use a no-gift control and one chosen metric (annual
selection, second/third payment retention, or conversion); do not infer the
other outcomes from a positive result.

**Agent updates:**

- No open update.

### [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-006--game-break-web-mindfulness-challenge-via-applovin|GH-006]] — Game-break reset

**Decision:** Is the post-game-break moment a viable acquisition route for a
brief MindfulText reset?

**Pass / fail signal:** a viable cost per defined second action, compared
between benefit-led creatives. A click or one-time challenge completion fails
to establish product demand.

**Agent updates:**

- Platform documentation establishes channel mechanics only, not MindfulText
  performance.

### [[growth/HYPOTHESES#gh-007--event-triggered-cold-outreach-to-seg-buyer-roles|GH-007]] — Event-triggered outreach

**Decision:** Does truthful public-event context increase meetings compared
with an untimed outreach baseline?

**Pass / fail signal:** scheduled conversations, measured separately from
positive replies, under a defined segment and buyer role. Neither establishes
product demand or purchase intent.

**Agent updates:**

- Blocked pending explicit Mark approval for any event monitoring or outreach.
