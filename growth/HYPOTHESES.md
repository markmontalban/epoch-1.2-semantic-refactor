### GH-002 — MindfulText for elevated-stress LCSWs

- Starting thought: **founder-assumption** — Licensed clinical social workers (LCSWs) can absorb the pain and traumatic material of people facing difficult mental-health issues; test whether MindfulText can help those who identify as experiencing high or elevated work stress.
- Product: MindfulText
- Segment ID: [[growth/SEGMENTS|SEG-001]]
- Segment: Care-delivery organizations (historical umbrella research; Run 002 will propose smaller atomic hypotheses where buyer, workflow, or GTM differs).
- ICP ID: [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-018]]
- ICP: **agent-hypothesis** — U.S. LCSWs who self-identify as experiencing elevated occupational stress while doing client-facing clinical work; they may buy individually or use an organization-sponsored program.
- Vertical / context: Clinical social work and community behavioral health; no client data, clinical diagnosis, or medical-treatment claim is in scope.
- Angle: **agent-hypothesis** — A brief, evidence-informed mindfulness practice program focused first on stress regulation and emotional exhaustion rather than a broad wellness or performance promise.
- GTM strategy: **agent-hypothesis** — Test three distinct routes without conflating them: direct LCSW users ([[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-018]]); non-MHA community behavioral-health organizations that may sponsor a team program ([[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-019]]); and LCSW associations/communities as cohort channels ([[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-020]]). The existing Mental Health America of Dutchess County relationship is a proof source, not a new-target pool.
- GTM IDs: [[growth/GTM-MOTIONS|GTM-001]] (account-led segment intelligence; Run 002)
- Pain: **founder-assumption** — Client-facing LCSWs may carry difficult client experiences and experience elevated stress. **agent-hypothesis** — secondary traumatic stress, emotional exhaustion, and compassion fatigue may make a short between-session or daily practice valuable; this is not assumed to be universal or solely an individual problem.
- Signals: **founder-assumption** — Mental Health America of Dutchess County has reportedly been a paying MindfulText customer for more than two years, with a case study and surveys indicating lower stress and improved organizational outcomes. The assets are not present in this run; treat them as unverified, non-generalizable proof until imported and spot-checked under [[decisions/2026-07-11-run-001-approvals|DR-13]]/[[decisions/2026-07-12-mark-directions|DR-18]].
- Offer-Outcome: **agent-hypothesis** — “MindfulText for LCSW stress reduction”: test perceived-stress and emotional-exhaustion outcomes first. Explore self-compassion, emotional regulation, focus/mental clarity, and resilience only as separately measured program hypotheses. Do not claim the broader screenshot outcomes—such as cognitive performance, leadership, physical strength, disease resistance, or organizational outcomes—as established for LCSWs without direct evidence.
- Research: auto
- Profile tracking: watch
- Status: exploring

- Segment ID: [[growth/SEGMENTS|SEG-002]]
- Segment: Community behavioral-health providers, a child of Care-delivery organizations.
- ICP ID: [[growth/ICP-REGISTRY|ICP-019]]
- ICP: **agent-hypothesis** — Organization-side workforce, clinical, operations, or quality buyer group at a community behavioral-health provider with staff-facing workforce/retention responsibilities.
- Vertical / context: Community behavioral health, crisis response, recovery, and related care delivery. MindfulText is a nonclinical complement to structural workforce interventions; it is not treatment or a remedy for staffing, workload, pay, or supervision problems.
- Angle: **agent-hypothesis** — A daily, asynchronous, text-based reset that fits a one-to-two-minute workday window may be a lower-disruption employee benefit than workshops or app-dependent programming.

- Offer-Outcome: **agent-hypothesis** — Test whether a buyer sees a daily text-based staff-support benefit as sufficiently low-lift and relevant to discuss a bounded pilot design; do not claim stress, retention, pride, quality, or organizational outcomes until separately evidenced.

- Starting thought: **founder-assumption** — A short web-based game or challenge that helps a person practice mindfulness may fit the moment immediately after a mobile-game break; AppLovin could test whether that moment creates useful discovery for MindfulText.
- Product: MindfulText web experience — a 10–30 second, nonclinical mindfulness challenge or “reset,” with an optional path into the broader product.
- Segment ID:
- Segment: **agent-hypothesis** — Adult mobile-game players reached through AppLovin mobile-game inventory; do not treat the audience as a single demographic or as already interested in mindfulness.
- ICP ID:
- ICP: **agent-hypothesis** — A mobile-game player willing to try a brief, low-friction attention or pause challenge after a game interruption; repeat use and signup intent are unknown.
- Vertical / context: Direct-to-consumer web acquisition. The destination is a web app; the likely ad environment is another publisher’s mobile game. The experience is not clinical care, diagnosis, or crisis support.
- Angle: **agent-hypothesis** — Sell an immediate, playable “pause/reset” rather than mindfulness in the abstract, using the transition between game play and the next activity as the entry moment.
- GTM strategy: **agent-hypothesis** — Run a bounded AppLovin web-advertising experiment comparing two or more benefit-led creative concepts and send users to the same short web challenge. Optimize beyond clicks only when a reliable downstream event is available; assess initial challenge completion, return/second-challenge use, and voluntary signup separately.
- GTM IDs:
- Pain: **agent-hypothesis** — A game break may create a small opening for a quick reset or attention exercise, but it does not by itself demonstrate mindfulness pain, need, or willingness to pay.
- Signals: **agent-research** — AppLovin says its web-ad platform buys measurable outcomes, including ROAS, cost per purchaser, and leads, and reaches users primarily through mobile-game inventory ([platform announcement](https://www.applovin.com/en/blog/applovin-ads-now-open)). These describe channel mechanics, not MindfulText performance.
- Offer-Outcome: **agent-hypothesis** — Test whether an immediate web challenge can earn a meaningful second action (return, second challenge, or signup) at a viable acquisition cost. Do not treat an ad click or one-time completion as product-market fit or willingness to pay.
