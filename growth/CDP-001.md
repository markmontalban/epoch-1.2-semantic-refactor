# CDP-001 — Care-delivery customer development plan

Last updated: 2026-07-14 00:38:29 PDT — edited by: Codex

`CDP-001` is the reusable customer-development plan for `SEG-001 —
Care-delivery organizations`. Its first focused branch is `SEG-002 — Community
behavioral-health providers`; it links to `GH-004` and `GTM-001`. The plan
organizes learning and opportunity mapping. It does not validate demand,
create a CRM, monitor people, send messages, or authorize marketing activity.

## What this plan is for

- **Find qualified accounts:** Identify care-delivery organizations with public
  workforce context, a plausible buyer group, and a potential low-disruption
  support gap—not presumed purchase intent.
- **Learn market language:** Capture how organizations, category bodies, and
  adjacent sellers describe workforce strain, retention, wellbeing, staffing,
  workflow, and employee support.
- **Surface marketing opportunities:** Map owned-content themes, category
  conversations, partners, and competitor positioning worth considering later;
  no content, partnership, or campaign is launched by this plan.
- **Earn discovery authorization:** Turn only sufficiently grounded account
  evidence into a separate, Mark-reviewed manual discovery-test proposal.

## Stage A — Target and account intelligence

| Work item | Starting scope | Evidence to retain | Decision it supports |

The next execution must be a separately bounded public-research run. It may
use public pages and cited sources only; no scraping, ongoing monitoring,
contact enrichment, or outreach is permitted.

## Stage B — Marketing opportunity map

- **Problem-language themes:** Collect recurring, attributable terms that may
  inform future owned content or conversations, such as workforce resilience,
  staff wellbeing, retention, burnout, workload, and low-downtime care.
- **Audience and channel hypotheses:** Distinguish organization buyers from
  possible amplifiers—associations, workforce programs, benefits partners, or
  category publications. An amplifier is not treated as a buyer by default.
- **Opportunity record:** For each candidate, record the audience, public
  evidence, relevant language, proposed value angle, contradiction, and the
  cheapest next learning step. Do not draft, publish, pitch, or contact.
- **Claim boundary:** MindfulText may be framed only as a nonclinical,
  low-disruption support complement. It must not be presented as a fix for
  staffing, pay, workload, supervision, retention, or clinical outcomes.

## Stage C — Advancement rules

| Evidence state | CDP decision | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At least two accounts have current public workforce context, a plausible buyer-role path, and a specific unresolved support/workflow question. | Advance to a draft manual-discovery packet. | Define one level-2 learning conversation, its questions, and pass/fail/stop conditions for Mark review. |
| Public material shows wellness is clearly redundant, structurally mismatched, or lacks any plausible owner. | Revise or park the focused hypothesis/account. | Record the contradiction; compare another `SEG-001` branch only if it could change the next decision. |
| Evidence is only broad category interest or historic testimonials. | Do not advance. | Continue bounded research or stop; neither is buyer intent. |

Mark must separately approve every manual conversation, marketing experiment,
partnership approach, or new research run. [DR-31](../decisions/2026-07-14-customer-development-plan.md)
