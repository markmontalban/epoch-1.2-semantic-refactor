# WF-007 — Product-concept validation

- **Inputs:** product opportunity, audience, alternatives, capability constraints, and decision axes.
- **Outputs:** bounded concept, measurement plan, risk boundaries, sequencing, and a build/test recommendation.
- **Gates:** separate market, access, claims, privacy, and technical axes rather than blending them.
- **Stop:** no testable user value, unsupported health claim, unbounded data need, or missing comparison.
- **Replay:** refresh evidence; fork when the concept, audience, or primary decision changes.
