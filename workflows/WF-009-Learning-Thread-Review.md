# WF-009 — Learning-thread review

- **Inputs:** one `TRK-###`, its subjects, runs, decisions, evidence, unknowns, and prior state.
- **Outputs:** dated review, changed state, confidence, gaps, next decision, and promotion/park recommendation.
- **Gates:** distinguish what changed from what was merely repeated; cite every promotion or reversal.
- **Stop:** no new evidence or decision consequence; record “no change” rather than manufacturing movement.
- **Replay:** every review is dated; use the same thread and link the previous review.

This workflow is the reusable successor to ad hoc per-segment lookbacks.
